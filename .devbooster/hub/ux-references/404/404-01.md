# Reference — Minimalist left-aligned 404

- image: 404-01.png
- type: 404 error page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A direct, minimalist error page explaining that the requested route does not exist or has moved, with options to return to the previous page or go home.

## Route Layout Blueprint

Content is arranged in a narrow left-aligned column with generous empty space around it. A small error label appears above a large title, followed by a short explanation and a row of two actions.

## Sections (top → bottom)

- error identification: small 404 label above the main content.
- main message: prominent title followed by one explanatory sentence.
- local actions: two side-by-side buttons; back is secondary and home is more prominent.

## Components

- error label: 1 text indicator.
- title: 1 primary heading.
- supporting text: 1 paragraph.
- actions: 2 buttons, including a secondary back action with an icon and a primary action.

## Density and Rhythm

Very spacious, with minimal information and broad vertical and horizontal breathing room.

## Observed States

Filled 404 error; no loading, form, or additional content.

## Observed Interaction Patterns on the Route

Both buttons appear clickable: go back and go to the home page.

## Observed Style (qualitative)

Clean, editorial, and highly reduced.

## Ideal For

Institutional websites, SaaS products, and applications that need simple route recovery.

## Do Not Reproduce

Proprietary visible copy and textual identity; use equivalent generic content.

## Overview

A two-column error page with the message and actions on the left and a tall editorial photograph on the right.

## Route Layout Blueprint

The functional column contains a title, explanation, and two side-by-side buttons. The right column presents a photograph occupying most of the available height, acting as atmosphere rather than interactive content.

## Sections (top → bottom)

- message: left-aligned title and description.
- actions: secondary back button and primary home button.
- image: tall photographic panel on the right.

## Components

- heading: 1.
- paragraph: 1.
- actions: 2 buttons.
- photograph: 1 decorative image.

## Density and Rhythm

Spacious and calm, with the text block vertically centered within its column.

## Observed States

Filled 404 error with no search or loading state.

## Observed Interaction Patterns on the Route

Back and home actions.

## Observed Style (qualitative)

Editorial, human, and asymmetric.

## Ideal For

Content sites, portfolios, consultancies, and products with strong visual communication.

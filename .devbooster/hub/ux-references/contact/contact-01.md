# Reference — Split contact form with portrait

- image: contact-01.png
- type: contact form page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A friendly contact route that combines a direct inquiry form with a large editorial portrait. The primary goal is to let visitors submit a message while reinforcing a human, approachable brand impression.

## Route layout blueprint

The route uses a two-column composition. The left column contains the route title, supporting description, and a stacked contact form; the right column contains one tall portrait image aligned with the form area. A local form action appears at the bottom of the left column.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Intro and form: heading, short supporting text, two compact name fields in one row, then full-width email, phone, and message fields.
- Consent and action: one privacy-policy checkbox followed by a wide submit button.
- Supporting visual: one large portrait panel occupying the full height of the form section on the right.

## Components

- Route heading and subtitle: one primary heading with one supporting sentence.
- Name inputs: two side-by-side required text fields.
- Email input: one required full-width field.
- Phone input: one optional field with country selector and number input.
- Message textarea: one required multiline field.
- Consent checkbox: one required agreement control.
- Submit button: one prominent local form action.
- Editorial image: one tall portrait image; the specific person and artwork are intentionally not reproduced.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and balanced, with generous separation between the introductory copy, form groups, and image. The form itself is orderly and moderately dense.

## Observed states

Empty form state with visible placeholders; no validation, loading, error, or submission feedback is shown.

## Observed route interactions

Text inputs, a country selector, a multiline message field, a consent checkbox, and a prominent submit action appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, contemporary, editorial contact layout with a warm human emphasis and strong split-screen hierarchy.

## Ideal for

Agency, consultancy, studio, or service-business contact pages that want a personal visual alongside a low-friction inquiry form.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore the global navigation and footer. Do not reproduce the visible product name, proprietary form copy, portrait, background artwork, logos, or brand-specific wording.

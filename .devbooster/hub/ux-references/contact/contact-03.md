# Reference — Contact form with information sidebar

- image: contact-03.png
- type: contact inquiry page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A two-panel inquiry route pairing contact details with a comprehensive form. The goal is to give visitors immediate support, office, and phone information while collecting project requirements.

## Route layout blueprint

The route is split vertically. A narrow information panel occupies the left side with an introduction and three stacked contact methods; the wider right side contains a centered form block with heading, supporting line, fields, service selections, and a submit action.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Contact information panel: introduction followed by chat, office, and phone blocks, each with an icon, description, and contact detail.
- Inquiry form: heading and supporting line, two name fields in one row, email, phone, message, and a two-column service checklist.
- Form action: one wide submit button below the service options.

## Components

- Information blocks: three stacked blocks for chat, office, and phone.
- Iconography: one contextual icon for each information block.
- Name inputs: two required fields side by side.
- Email and phone inputs: full-width fields, with phone including a country selector.
- Message textarea: one multiline field.
- Service checklist: six checkbox options arranged in two columns.
- Submit button: one prominent local action.

## Density and rhythm

The information panel is compact and vertically stacked, while the form is spacious and highly structured. The split creates a clear contrast between quick-reference details and the longer interaction.

## Observed states

Empty form with placeholders and unchecked service options. No validation, loading, error, or success state is shown.

## Observed route interactions

All form fields, the phone selector, six service checkboxes, and the submit button appear interactive. Contact details may be actionable links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

High-contrast, modern service-contact layout with a strong informational sidebar and conversion-oriented form.

## Ideal for

Digital agencies, consultancies, studios, and professional services collecting qualified project inquiries.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce global navigation, product identity, exact contact information, proprietary text, logos, or the specific sidebar treatment; describe only the structural relationship.

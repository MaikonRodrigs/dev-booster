# Reference — Contact channels followed by form

- image: contact-09.png
- type: contact overview and inquiry page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A long-form contact route that first offers three quick contact channels and then guides visitors into a detailed inquiry form. The structure supports both immediate communication and more considered project requests.

## Route layout blueprint

The top section is centered with an eyebrow, heading, and subtitle, followed by three evenly spaced contact blocks for email, office, and phone. After a large vertical gap, a second centered heading introduces a narrow contact form that continues below the visible viewport.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Contact channels: centered intro followed by three columns, each with a circular icon, title, short description, and contact detail.
- Inquiry form introduction: a second centered label, heading, and supporting sentence.
- Inquiry form: narrow two-column name row followed by stacked email, phone, and message fields; lower controls continue beyond the visible image.

## Components

- Channel blocks: three equal-width summaries for email, office, and phone.
- Channel icons: one icon per summary.
- Form heading: one secondary introduction block.
- Name inputs: two required fields side by side.
- Email, phone, and message controls: stacked form fields.

## Density and rhythm

Very spacious and vertically paced, with substantial separation between the quick-contact row and detailed form. The upper area is light and scannable.

## Observed states

Contact channels are populated; the form is empty with placeholders. The full form submission state is not visible.

## Observed route interactions

Email, office, phone, and form controls appear interactive. No filters, tabs, or local selectors are visible besides the phone selector.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, centered, and editorial, with a strong two-stage contact journey.

## Ideal for

Organizations that want visitors to choose between immediate contact methods and a more detailed project inquiry.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global navigation. Do not reproduce exact contact details, proprietary text, product identity, logos, specific icons, or brand-specific wording.

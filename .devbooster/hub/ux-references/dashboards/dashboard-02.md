## Overview

A traffic-analysis route combining headline engagement metrics with a detailed pages-and-screens table.

## Route layout blueprint

The route title and local actions appear above internal traffic tabs. Three metric cards with miniature line charts form the first row. A large table section follows with visibility tabs, search, filters, sortable columns, progress indicators, and pagination.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Traffic header: title, dashboard switch, export action, and traffic-segment tabs.
- Summary metrics: three cards for sessions, duration, and pages per session.
- Pages table: a full-width tabular report with controls and pagination.

## Components

- Metric cards: three cards with values, comparisons, and sparklines.
- Tabs: six traffic views plus table visibility tabs.
- Data table: rows with page, sessions, average time, percentage, and grouping.
- Controls: search, filters, row actions, and pagination.

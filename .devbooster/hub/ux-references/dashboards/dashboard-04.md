# Reference — Revenue and customer dashboard

- image: dashboard-04.png
- type: revenue analytics dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A workspace dashboard focused on revenue, page views, active users, and a customer directory.

## Route layout blueprint

Local breadcrumbs and route actions sit above three summary cards. A wide net-revenue line chart follows, then a full-width customer table with search, sortable columns, statuses, amounts, and pagination.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Route header: local context, title, and actions.
- Summary row: three KPI cards.
- Revenue trend: one large two-series line chart.
- Customer directory: searchable table with selection, contact details, date, status, amount, and actions.

## Components

- KPI cards: three.
- Line chart: one wide comparison chart.
- Customer table: multiple rows with avatars and statuses.
- Controls: period tabs, filters, search, sorting, row actions, and pagination.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced, with a prominent chart and dense but readable table.

## Observed states

Populated customer records and metrics; no empty, loading, or error state.

## Observed route interactions

Period selector, filters, search, table selection, sorting, edit/delete actions, and pagination.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean SaaS administration dashboard.

## Ideal for

B2B workspaces, customer-success tools, and revenue reporting.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore sidebar, verification widgets, account identity, exact values, company names, logos, avatars, and proprietary text.

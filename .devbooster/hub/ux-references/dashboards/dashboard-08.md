# Reference — Vendor risk overview

- image: dashboard-08.png
- type: vendor risk dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

An organization-level dashboard for comparing vendor ratings and reviewing vendor assessment records.

## Route layout blueprint

A title and local filter/customize/export actions sit above two analytics cards: a donut breakdown and a wide rating comparison chart. A vendor-movement table occupies the lower half.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: title and local controls.
- Vendor analytics: donut breakdown beside a line comparison chart.
- Vendor movements: searchable table with rating bars, changes, assessment dates, categories, and pagination.

## Components

- Donut chart: one vendor-rating distribution.
- Line chart: one comparison chart.
- Vendor table: multiple rows with selection, ratings, change badges, dates, category tags, and actions.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced but data-dense, with two clear analytics cards followed by a large table.

## Observed states

Populated ratings and vendor records; no loading, empty, or error state.

## Observed route interactions

Filters, customize, export, view report, search, row selection, category badges, menus, and pagination.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Enterprise risk and compliance dashboard.

## Ideal for

Vendor management, security review, procurement, and compliance tools.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global navigation, exact vendors, logos, ratings, dates, names, proprietary wording, and promotional elements.

# Reference — Sales overview with activity timeline

- image: dashboard-11.png
- type: sales overview dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A sales overview focused on long-term performance, daily KPI cards, and a chronological recent-activity feed.

## Route layout blueprint

A title and date controls sit above one large comparison line chart. Three KPI cards follow, then a two-column recent-activity section with timeline-style event lists and local download/view-all actions.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header and controls: title, period selector, date range, and filters.
- Sales trend: one wide multi-series line chart.
- KPI row: three metric cards with miniature trend indicators.
- Recent activity: two side-by-side event columns.

## Components

- Main chart: one wide comparison line chart.
- KPI cards: three.
- Activity lists: two columns of purchase events with avatars and timestamps.
- Actions: download and view-all controls.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and readable, with generous separation between analytics and activity.

## Observed states

Populated chart and activity records; no loading, error, or empty state.

## Observed route interactions

Period controls, date range, filters, download, view-all, and activity links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Editorial sales dashboard with a timeline-oriented lower section.

## Ideal for

Revenue reporting, sales operations, and executive summaries.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell, exact data, identities, avatars, product names, logos, and proprietary copy.

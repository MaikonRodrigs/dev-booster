## Overview

A personal finance route showing account balance trends, transaction history, cards, and budget progress.

## Route layout blueprint

The main area starts with a balance summary and a wide trend chart. Below is a transaction table with date and filter controls. A right-side finance panel contains profile context, payment cards, monthly spending progress, and budgets.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Balance overview: total balance, account-type selector, and line chart.
- Transaction history: searchable or filtered table of transactions.
- Finance side panel: profile summary, cards, monthly progress, and budget cards.

## Components

- Balance chart: one wide line chart.
- Transaction table: multiple rows with merchant, amount, date, category, account, and edit action.
- Payment cards: a horizontal card carousel or card pair.
- Budget cards: three colored progress modules.

## Density and rhythm

Information-dense, with a strong primary table and a compact financial side panel.

## Observed states

Populated balances, transactions, cards, and budgets; no loading, error, or empty state.

## Observed route interactions

Account selector, date range, filters, transaction edits, card carousel, budget modules, and pagination.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Friendly consumer-finance dashboard with strong utility focus.

## Ideal for

Personal banking, expense tracking, budgeting, and financial wellness products.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell, profile identity, card numbers, merchant names, exact financial values, logos, and proprietary text.

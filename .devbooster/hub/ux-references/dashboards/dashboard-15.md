# Reference — Monthly spending dashboard

- image: dashboard-15.png
- type: spending analytics dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A card-spending route showing recent transactions, category distribution, balance trends, and monthly spending totals.

## Route layout blueprint

A narrow left column contains payment-card previews and a recent-transactions list. The wider right column contains a donut/category summary, a balance line chart, and a spending bar chart.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Card and transactions rail: card previews followed by a vertical recent-transaction list.
- Monthly spending: donut chart with category totals and period controls.
- Balance trend: one multi-series line chart.
- Spending trend: one stacked bar chart.

## Components

- Card previews: two overlapping payment cards.
- Transaction list: approximately ten entries with amount, timestamp, and edit action.
- Donut chart: one category distribution chart.
- Line chart: one balance comparison chart.
- Bar chart: one monthly spending chart.

## Density and rhythm

Dense and analytical, balancing a highly scannable transaction rail with large charts.

## Observed states

Populated transactions and charts; no loading, error, or empty state.

## Observed route interactions

Card actions, transaction edits, period controls, chart menus, and view-all-transactions action.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Consumer-finance analytics with strong visual reporting.

## Ideal for

Budgeting, personal finance, and card-spend tracking products.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell, card numbers, merchant brands, names, exact values, logos, and proprietary text.

- image: dashboard-17.png

- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A marketing analytics route for tracking users, sessions, session duration, geographic activity, acquisition sources, and channel distribution.

## Route layout blueprint

Three KPI cards sit above a large active-user world map with a ranked geographic breakdown. A lower section pairs a stacked acquisition chart with a donut chart for source distribution.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- KPI header: three metrics with miniature trend indicators, period controls, date range, and local actions.
- Active users map: world map visualization with markers and country breakdown.
- Acquisition analysis: stacked monthly bar chart beside a source donut chart.

## Components

- KPI cards: three for users, sessions, and duration.
- World map: one geographic activity visualization.
- Country ranking: approximately five countries with progress bars.
- Bar chart: one acquisition trend chart.
- Donut chart: one source distribution chart.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and visual, with the map as the dominant middle section and compact supporting analytics below.

## Observed states

Populated real-time and historical analytics; no loading, error, or empty state.

## Observed route interactions

Period/date controls, filters, export/insights, real-time report, location report, and chart controls.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Modern marketing and audience analytics dashboard.

## Ideal for

Growth, advertising, product marketing, and website analytics teams.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell and referral widget; omit countries, values, exact copy, logos, flags, and proprietary wording.

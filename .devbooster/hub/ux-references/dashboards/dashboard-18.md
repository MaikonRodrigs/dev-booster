The route begins with an overview row of four metrics and a wide line chart, paired with a radar-style traffic-source chart. Below, a channel acquisition section combines a stacked bar chart with a large country donut chart.

- Acquisition section: channel/source/referral tabs, stacked bar chart, and country donut.

- Metric summaries: four.
- Line chart: one multi-series trend.
- Radar chart: one traffic-source comparison.
- Stacked bar chart: one acquisition chart.
- Donut chart: one country distribution chart.
- Internal tabs: three acquisition views.

## Density and rhythm

Compact and analytical, with two dense reporting bands and minimal decorative content.

Populated analytics throughout; no loading, error, or empty state.

Time range tabs, custom range, audience overview actions, acquisition tabs, chart menus, and local report actions.

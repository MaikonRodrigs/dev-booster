## Overview

A lightweight onboarding email welcoming a new user and directing them toward account access. It establishes a simple branded email structure with navigation, greeting, reassurance, one primary action, and compliance footer.

## Route layout blueprint

A top brand area is followed by a horizontal navigation row. The body contains a personalized greeting, two short welcome paragraphs, a sign-off, and one prominent login button. A legal footer contains delivery preferences, unsubscribe links, address information, and social links.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Email header: brand mark/name and four navigation links.
- Welcome message: greeting, two short paragraphs, and sign-off.
- Primary action: one login button.
- Compliance footer: preference/unsubscribe text, mailing address, and social links.

## Components

- Header: one brand area and four navigation items.
- Message block: greeting, two paragraphs, and sign-off.
- CTA: one primary button.
- Footer: legal copy, three preference links, address, and three social icons.

## Density and rhythm

Very spacious and minimal, with large breathing room between the message, CTA, and footer.

## Observed states

Static welcome email; no loading, error, progress, or empty state.

## Observed route interactions

Navigation links, login CTA, unsubscribe, preference management, and social links appear clickable.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, friendly, editorial email with a strong text-first hierarchy.

## Ideal for

Account welcome emails, SaaS onboarding, membership confirmation, and product introductions.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the brand, logo, names, exact copy, email addresses, dates, address, social identities, or proprietary wording.

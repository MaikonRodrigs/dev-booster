## Overview

A focused verification email presenting a short-lived code and a fallback verification action. Its primary goal is to complete sign-in or email ownership verification quickly.

## Route layout blueprint

A simple brand header appears above a greeting and verification explanation. A prominent row of four separate code cells is followed by expiration guidance, one verification button, a sign-off, and a compliance footer.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: centered or left-aligned brand area.
- Verification content: greeting and explanation.
- Code display: four large individual code cells in one row.
- Fallback action: expiration note and one verify-email button.
- Footer: preference links, address, and social links.

## Components

- Code cells: four visually separated characters.
- CTA: one verification button.
- Footer: legal delivery text, unsubscribe/preferences links, address, and social icons.

## Density and rhythm

Compact around the code, with generous spacing before and after the verification action.

## Observed states

Active verification state with an expiring code; no error or loading feedback is shown.

## Observed route interactions

Verification button, unsubscribe, preference management, and social links appear clickable. The code itself may be copied or entered elsewhere.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Utility-first, calm, and highly focused transactional email.

## Ideal for

Passwordless login, email verification, two-factor authentication, and account activation.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the brand, actual code, names, exact expiration text, addresses, social identities, or proprietary copy.

- image: email-05.webp

## Overview

A welcome email using a wide abstract hero image to create visual emphasis before a short onboarding message and login action.

## Route layout blueprint

A simple brand header appears first, followed by one wide decorative image. The body then presents a greeting, two welcome paragraphs, a sign-off, and one login button. A compact legal and social footer follows.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: brand area.
- Hero visual: one wide abstract image.
- Welcome copy: greeting, two paragraphs, and sign-off.
- CTA: one login button.
- Footer: delivery preferences, unsubscribe/manage links, address, and social icons.

## Components

- Hero image: one wide decorative visual.
- Message block: greeting, two paragraphs, and sign-off.
- CTA: one primary login action.
- Footer: compliance text, links, address, and three social icons.

## Density and rhythm

Moderately spacious, with the visual separating the header from the text-first message.

## Observed states

Static welcome state; no loading, error, or progress feedback.

## Observed route interactions

Login, unsubscribe, preference management, and social links appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Warm, visual, and editorial onboarding email.

## Ideal for

Brand-led welcome campaigns, creative products, and new-account activation.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the abstract image, brand, exact text, names, address, social identities, or proprietary wording.

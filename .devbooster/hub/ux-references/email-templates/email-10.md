## Overview

A product announcement email introducing a digital product through a laptop/device visual, a welcome message, a direct download action, and mobile-app promotion.

## Route layout blueprint

A top header combines the brand on the left with login and social links on the right. A large device mockup visual precedes the main heading and two welcome paragraphs. A primary download button follows, then a secondary app-download section and a legal footer.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: brand, login, and social links.
- Product visual: one large laptop/device image.
- Introduction: heading, two paragraphs, and sign-off.
- Primary action: one download button.
- App promotion: heading, supporting line, and two platform badges.
- Footer: delivery preferences, unsubscribe/manage links, address, and social icons.

## Components

- Device hero: one large product visual.
- Message block: heading, two paragraphs, and sign-off.
- Primary CTA: one download action.
- App badges: two secondary download actions.
- Footer: legal and social elements.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious but content-rich, with the device visual creating a strong opening and clear CTA sequence.

## Observed states

Static product introduction; no loading, error, or progress state.

## Observed route interactions

Login, social links, download CTA, app-store badges, unsubscribe, and preference management.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Modern, promotional, and product-led.

## Ideal for

Software launches, desktop-app announcements, cross-platform products, and activation campaigns.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce device screenshots, brand, product name, exact copy, app-store marks, email/address details, social identities, or proprietary visuals.

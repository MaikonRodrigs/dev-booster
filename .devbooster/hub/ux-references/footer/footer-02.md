A light footer combining a brand introduction, social-proof chip, two navigation groups, and an email subscription form.

The upper row has brand copy on the left, two link columns in the center, and a newsletter heading, input, and button on the right. A divider leads to copyright and legal links.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Footer content: brand summary, social-proof element, two link groups, and newsletter form.
- Bottom bar: copyright on the left and legal links on the right.

## Components

- Brand block: logo/brand, description, and social-proof chip.
- Link groups: two.
- Newsletter form: email input and subscribe button.
- Legal bar: copyright and three links.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious.

## Observed states

Empty newsletter input; no validation or success state.

## Observed route interactions

Navigation links, email input, subscribe button, legal links, and social-proof chip.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Friendly, polished, and conversion-aware.

## Ideal for

SaaS and creative-product marketing sites.

## Overview

A light footer preceded by a horizontal trial call-to-action.

The top band places a CTA heading and supporting line on the left, with secondary and primary buttons on the right. A divider leads into a five-column navigation footer and a final social/legal row.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- CTA band: heading, description, and two actions.
- Navigation footer: brand summary plus five link groups.
- Bottom bar: copyright and social icons.

## Components

- CTA: two buttons.
- Link groups: five.
- Brand block: one.
- Social group: one.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced with clear section separation.

## Observed states

Static CTA and populated links.

## Observed route interactions

CTA buttons, navigation links, and social links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Conversion-oriented and polished.

SaaS landing pages and trial-driven products.

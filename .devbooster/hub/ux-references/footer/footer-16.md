## Overview

A light footer with a newsletter band, social-proof chip, navigation columns, and brand/copyright base.

## Route layout blueprint

Newsletter heading and form appear at the top. A divider leads to the link grid with brand content on the left, followed by a lower brand/copyright row.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Newsletter band: heading, description, input, and subscribe action.
- Navigation grid: brand/social proof plus five link groups.
- Bottom bar: brand mark and copyright.

## Components

- Newsletter form: one.
- Social-proof chip: one.
- Link groups: five.
- Bottom brand/copyright: one row.

## Density and rhythm

Rich but clearly segmented.

## Observed states

Empty newsletter input.

## Observed route interactions

Subscription, navigation, and social-proof links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean and conversion-oriented.

## Ideal for

Content-driven product sites.

## Do not reproduce

Omit brand, avatars, exact copy, logos, and proprietary labels.

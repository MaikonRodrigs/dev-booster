## Overview

A full dark footer combining newsletter signup, brand/trust content, five link groups, and social/legal controls.

## Route layout blueprint

Newsletter row comes first. Below it, brand copy and trust badge occupy the left while five link columns fill the rest. A final social/copyright bar closes the footer.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Newsletter band: title, description, input, and subscribe button.
- Main grid: brand/trust block and five link groups.
- Bottom bar: copyright and social icons.

## Components

- Newsletter form: one.
- Trust badge: one.
- Link groups: five.
- Social group: one.

## Density and rhythm

Rich and layered.

## Observed states

Empty newsletter field.

## Observed route interactions

Subscription, navigation, and social links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Dark, comprehensive, and marketing-led.

## Ideal for

Large product and SaaS websites.

## Do not reproduce

Omit brand, badge, exact copy, social identities, and logos.

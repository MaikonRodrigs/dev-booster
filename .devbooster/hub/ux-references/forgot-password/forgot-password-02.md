## Overview

A focused account-recovery route where a user submits an email address to receive password reset instructions.

## Route layout blueprint

A single narrow form is centered in the route. From top to bottom it includes a recovery icon, heading, supporting explanation, one labeled email field, a full-width reset action, and a secondary return-to-login link.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Recovery introduction: one compact key-style icon, heading, and supporting line.
- Email entry: one labeled email input with placeholder guidance.
- Form action: one prominent full-width reset-password button.
- Secondary navigation: one inline back-to-login link with a directional icon.

## Components

- Recovery icon: one generic password-recovery symbol.
- Heading: one forgot-password title.
- Supporting text: one short instruction explaining that reset guidance will be sent.
- Email field: one text input for the account email address.
- Primary button: one reset-password submission action.
- Back link: one secondary link returning to authentication.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and highly focused. The compact form has consistent vertical grouping and substantial surrounding whitespace.

## Observed states

Empty recovery form with no entered email. No validation, loading, error, or success feedback is visible.

## Observed route interactions

The email field, reset button, and back-to-login link appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, centered, and reassuring authentication experience with a clear single-task flow.

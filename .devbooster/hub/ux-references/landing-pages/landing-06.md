# Reference — Business Card Platform Landing Page

## Overview

A conversion-focused landing page for a business card and spend-management platform. It introduces the service, explains core benefits, demonstrates a web dashboard, presents pricing, and closes with trial and newsletter calls to action.

## Route layout blueprint

A compact top hero pairs a large value proposition and local actions with an oversized angled card composition. A trust strip follows, then a feature section with explanatory copy and a card visual. A dashboard showcase appears next, followed by a centered trial banner, a second feature-and-pricing sequence, a prominent trial panel, and newsletter signup.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Hero: large heading, supporting paragraph, two local actions, and a wide decorative card arrangement.
- Trust strip: centered social-proof statement with a row of partner marks, described generically.
- Feature introduction: small section label, prominent heading, short explanation, and a vertical list of three benefits beside a large card visual.
- Product proof: centered heading and a wide embedded business dashboard preview with metrics and a line chart.
- Trial prompt: centered call to action with supporting text and two buttons.
- Pricing feature section: feature copy and a single pricing card with plan details and a local purchase action.
- Closing trial panel: wide emphasized banner with heading, supporting text, and two actions.
- Newsletter: email signup with supporting copy and a submit action.

## Components

- Hero heading and supporting copy: one primary message block.
- Local action buttons: two hero actions.
- Decorative payment-card composition: one large visual group.
- Trust indicator: one centered partner-logo row, not reproduced.
- Benefit list: three stacked feature items with short descriptions and links.
- Product dashboard preview: one large application screenshot containing summary metrics, controls, and a line chart; proprietary interface text omitted.
- Trial banners: two conversion components with paired actions.
- Pricing card: one plan comparison/purchase component.
- Newsletter form: one email field and submit action.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and highly sectional, with generous separation between marketing blocks. Content alternates between broad centered areas and two-column feature layouts, while the pricing and newsletter areas become more information-dense.

## Observed states

The route is populated and promotional. The dashboard is shown in a populated analytics state; no loading, error, empty, editing, or upload state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Hero buttons, feature links, trial actions, the pricing purchase action, and newsletter submission appear clickable. The dashboard includes visible local controls, but they are part of the illustrative product preview rather than the landing route.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, polished, conversion-oriented SaaS marketing layout with strong product imagery, alternating editorial sections, and clear calls to action.

## Ideal for

Business banking, corporate cards, expense management, spend controls, finance operations, and other B2B SaaS product launches.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the visible brand identity, partner logos, product names, proprietary marketing copy, dashboard labels, payment-card numbers, payment-network marks, or specific card artwork. Use generic text, neutral product imagery, and abstract interface content instead.

# Reference — Finance product landing page with layered card hero

- type: fintech SaaS landing page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A conversion-focused finance product landing page for businesses and teams that need account management, payment cards, reporting, and guided onboarding. The route combines a product-led hero, feature explanation, pricing comparison, social proof, and repeated calls to action.

## Route layout blueprint

The main route starts with a two-column hero: a text block and local actions on the left, and a large layered payment-card product visual on the right. It continues with a centered feature introduction and a six-item feature grid, followed by three pricing cards. A testimonial carousel appears next, then a customer-logo trust strip, a final onboarding section pairing text with a dashboard preview, and a prominent closing conversion panel before the route ends.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- hero: left-aligned announcement, primary heading, supporting paragraph, and two local actions beside a large product visual.
- feature overview: centered section label, heading, short explanation, and six feature items arranged in two rows of three.
- pricing: centered heading and supporting text above three side-by-side plan cards with plan labels, price, action, and feature lists.
- testimonials: heading and supporting line above a horizontally arranged set of customer testimonial cards with portraits, ratings, and short quotes.
- trust strip: a compact row of partner or customer marks used as social proof; the specific marks are omitted.
- onboarding CTA: text-led prompt with short benefit list and local actions beside an analytics dashboard preview.
- closing conversion: a high-emphasis full-width call to action with a short reassurance and two actions.

## Components

- hero content block: announcement, heading, paragraph, and two buttons.
- product illustration: one large abstract payment-card stack; specific artwork is not reproduced.
- feature grid: six icon-and-text items with short descriptions.
- pricing cards: three comparable cards, each with a price, short description, primary action, secondary action, and feature checklist.
- testimonial carousel: multiple portrait cards with rating indicators and customer quotes; specific people and copy are omitted.
- trust strip: one horizontal social-proof group.
- dashboard preview: one product interface image used to support the onboarding message; the interface content is not reproduced.
- closing CTA: one prominent message and two actions.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious, with generous separation between major sections. The page alternates centered text, structured grids, large imagery, and card groups to create a steady marketing rhythm without becoming information-dense.

## Observed states

The route is shown in a populated, default marketing state. Pricing is displayed as available plans, testimonials are populated, and no loading, error, empty, editing, or progress state is observed.

## Observed route interactions

The hero, pricing cards, onboarding area, and closing panel each expose apparent local CTA buttons. The testimonial area appears horizontally browsable, with carousel navigation controls. Pricing cards may support plan selection or sales contact actions.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, polished, product-led, and conversion-oriented. The composition uses restrained editorial spacing, soft interface mockups, rounded cards, and a strong accent treatment for actions and emphasis.

## Ideal for

Fintech products, business banking tools, expense platforms, payment services, and other SaaS products that need to explain capabilities while moving visitors toward trial or account creation.

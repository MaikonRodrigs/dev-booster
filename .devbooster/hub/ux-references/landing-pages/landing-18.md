# Reference — Customer service platform landing page

- image: landing-18.webp
- type: product-focused SaaS landing page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A product marketing page for customer service software, aimed at teams that need integrations, analytics, and measurable operational outcomes. The route leads from a split hero into an integration directory, an analytics showcase, proof metrics, a trial prompt, testimonial content, and a final product CTA.

## Route layout blueprint

The hero uses a two-column split with copy and local actions on the left and a rounded product-related image on the right. A proof strip follows, then a centered integrations section with six cards. A feature section presents a large dashboard preview, followed by three outcome metrics. A horizontal trial CTA, testimonial, split product CTA, and newsletter complete the route.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- hero: left-aligned heading, supporting paragraph, and two buttons beside one large rounded image.
- social proof: centered row of several generalized customer marks.
- integrations: centered heading and description above a two-by-three grid of six integration cards.
- analytics feature: centered feature heading and supporting copy above a large dashboard mockup.
- outcome metrics: centered icon or decorative cue, short explanation, and three prominent numeric metrics.
- trial CTA: horizontal light panel with a heading, supporting line, and two local actions.
- testimonial: centered customer quote with a small attribution.
- product CTA: left-aligned invitation and buttons beside a large dashboard preview.
- newsletter: compact email signup row with supporting description and submit action.

## Components

- split hero: 1 heading, 1 paragraph, 2 buttons, and 1 rounded image.
- proof strip: several generalized partner marks.
- integration cards: 6 equal cards in 2 rows, each with an icon, label, and short description.
- analytics showcase: 1 large product/dashboard mockup.
- metric row: 3 outcome metric blocks with labels.
- trial panel: 1 heading, 1 supporting line, and 2 buttons.
- testimonial: 1 quote and 1 attribution.
- product CTA visual: 1 text block, 2 buttons, and 1 dashboard mockup.
- newsletter form: 1 email field and 1 subscription button.

## Density and rhythm

Clean and structured with generous whitespace. The integration grid is the densest area, while the dashboard and testimonial sections act as broad visual pauses between conversion prompts.

## Observed states

Populated marketing route. Integration cards and analytics content are filled; the newsletter is an empty input state. No loading, error, upload, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Hero, trial, and product CTA buttons, integration cards, newsletter submission, and likely dashboard-related preview actions appear clickable. No route-local filters or tabs are visible.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, corporate, analytical, and product-led, with a restrained progression from explanation to proof to conversion.

## Ideal for

Customer support platforms, helpdesk software, analytics products, workflow tools, and integration-heavy B2B SaaS products.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce visible product names, logos, proprietary copy, customer marks, portraits, photographs, or exact dashboard artwork. Substitute generic integration labels, abstract interface mockups, and non-identifying imagery.

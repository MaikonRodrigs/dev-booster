# Reference — Feature-led customer service landing page

- type: feature-led SaaS marketing landing page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A product landing page focused on explaining how a customer service platform supports teams through automation, instant answers, reporting, and candidate or applicant workflows. It uses a visual hero, alternating feature rows, repeated trial prompts, testimonial proof, and a final product conversion block.

## Route layout blueprint

The hero is split between left-aligned copy with an email capture and right-aligned portrait imagery. A centered feature introduction leads into three alternating feature rows that pair concise explanatory text with dashboard or mobile-interface visuals. A trial panel interrupts the feature sequence, followed by a candidate-experience section, testimonial card, split CTA, newsletter, and dark closing CTA area.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- hero: large heading and supporting copy on the left, with an email field and primary action; a large portrait image occupies the right.
- feature introduction: centered label, heading, and short description.
- automation feature: left text block with three short benefit points and a dashboard visual on the right.
- instant answers feature: reversed split with a device visual on the left and text plus three benefit points on the right.
- reporting feature: text on the left and layered phone/dashboard visuals on the right, again with three benefit points.
- trial CTA: horizontal panel with a heading, supporting line, and two local actions.
- candidate experience: heading and description with a text feature list beside a large interface mockup.
- testimonial: wide card containing a quote, rating cue, attribution, and a supporting portrait image.
- product CTA: horizontal split with invitation and actions on the left and a dashboard image on the right.
- newsletter: compact email signup row.
- closing CTA: dark conversion panel with a heading, supporting text, and two actions.

## Components

- hero form: 1 email input and 1 primary submit button.
- hero visual: 1 large non-identifying portrait image.
- feature rows: 3 alternating two-column sections, each with a heading, paragraph, three benefit bullets, and a product or device visual.
- trial panel: 1 heading, 1 supporting line, and 2 buttons.
- candidate section: 1 heading, 1 paragraph, 3-item feature list, and 1 interface mockup.
- testimonial card: rating cue, quote, attribution, and 1 supporting image.
- product CTA: 1 text block, 2 buttons, and 1 dashboard preview.
- newsletter form: 1 email field and 1 submit action.
- closing CTA: 1 heading, 1 supporting line, and 2 buttons.

## Density and rhythm

Moderately spacious with a steady alternating rhythm. Feature rows are visually substantial and separated by generous whitespace; conversion panels create clear pauses and repeated opportunities to act.

## Observed states

The hero and newsletter forms are empty and ready for input. Feature content, testimonial content, and product previews are populated. No loading, error, upload, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

The hero and newsletter forms accept email input. Trial and CTA buttons appear clickable; feature visuals may link to product details. No local filters, tabs, or menus are visible.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, explanatory, polished, and conversion-oriented, with strong visual alternation and a slightly more direct sales narrative than an editorial agency page.

## Ideal for

Customer service software, automation platforms, recruiting workflows, support tools, and B2B products that need to explain several capabilities in sequence.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce visible brand names, proprietary copy, logos, exact ratings, portraits, photographs, or specific interface screenshots. Use generic feature language, abstract product visuals, and non-identifying imagery.

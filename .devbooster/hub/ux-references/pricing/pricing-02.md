## Overview

A transparent pricing route that combines three subscription cards with a six-item benefits grid, a detailed FAQ, and a prominent free-trial conversion banner.

## Route layout blueprint

The route opens with a left-aligned pricing heading and three cards. A feature section follows in a three-column by two-row grid, then a six-item FAQ grid and a wide question prompt. A contrasting trial banner closes the main content.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing header: section label, title, supporting copy, and three plans.
- Plan cards: three equal cards with billing note, feature checklist, primary action, and secondary sales action.
- Benefits grid: six icon-led feature descriptions arranged in two rows of three.
- FAQ grid: six questions and answers arranged in three columns across two rows.
- Contact prompt: one horizontal panel inviting further questions and a local contact action.
- Trial banner: a full-width free-trial CTA with supporting customer proof.

## Components

- Pricing cards: three tiers with checkmarked features and two actions each.
- Feature tiles: six benefit summaries with supporting links.
- FAQ entries: six static question-and-answer blocks.
- Contact CTA: one compact local support action.
- Trial banner: one conversion section with learn-more and start-trial actions.

## Density and rhythm

Moderately dense but highly structured; repeated three-column grids make the route easy to scan.

## Observed states

Fully populated route with no expanded accordion, loading, error, or checkout state visible.

## Observed route interactions

Plan actions, sales contact actions, feature links, and trial CTA buttons appear clickable.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, organized, and product-led SaaS pricing layout.

## Ideal for

B2B software, workflow platforms, analytics tools, and multi-tier service offerings.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce logos, brands, exact prices, proprietary copy, or global navigation/footer identity.

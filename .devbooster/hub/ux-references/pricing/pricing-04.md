## Overview

A straightforward pricing page emphasizing accessible plans, billing answers, a no-contract reassurance message, and a free-trial invitation.

## Route layout blueprint

The main route begins with a pricing introduction and three plan cards. A split FAQ section follows, then a two-column reassurance block pairing a bold message with a collage of people imagery, and a wide trial CTA.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing introduction: title, supporting copy, and three cards with tier features and actions.
- FAQ section: explanatory text on the left and an accordion list on the right.
- Reassurance block: large no-commitment message and supporting image collage.
- Trial CTA: horizontal free-trial banner with learn-more and start actions.

## Components

- Plan cards: three equal pricing cards with checklist features and primary buttons.
- FAQ accordion: approximately five questions with one expanded answer.
- Image collage: one grouped visual of people, intentionally non-specific.
- Trial CTA: one local conversion banner.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and conversion-focused, with clear pauses between pricing, reassurance, and CTA sections.

## Observed states

Populated cards and FAQ; one FAQ answer is open. No loading, error, form, or checkout state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

FAQ toggles, plan actions, and trial CTA buttons appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Premium, human-centered, and minimal pricing presentation.

## Ideal for

Service subscriptions, creative platforms, SaaS products, and flexible business plans.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the image collage, logos, brands, names, exact prices, proprietary copy, announcement strip, or global footer identity.

## Overview

A compact pricing route combining three plans with a detailed FAQ, a support prompt, customer testimonials, and a final free-trial CTA.

## Route layout blueprint

The route presents a centered title, billing toggle, and three cards with primary and sales actions. The FAQ is a centered accordion, followed by a question prompt, a six-card testimonial grid, and a trial CTA.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing header: title, supporting copy, and billing selector.
- Plan cards: three cards with prices, feature summaries, and paired actions.
- FAQ: centered heading and approximately five accordion entries.
- Support prompt: one wide box with customer avatars, supporting text, and contact action.
- Testimonials: six review cards arranged in three columns over two rows.
- Trial CTA: one horizontal free-trial banner.

## Components

- Billing toggle: one monthly/annual selector.
- Pricing cards: three tiers with popular-plan emphasis on one card.
- FAQ accordion: approximately five entries with one expanded answer.
- Support prompt: one contact CTA with avatar cluster.
- Review cards: six testimonial items with rating, quote, person, and organization marker.
- Trial CTA: one conversion banner.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and moderately dense, with repeated cards creating a clear scan pattern.

## Observed states

Populated route with one expanded FAQ answer; no loading, error, form, or payment state visible.

## Observed route interactions

Billing control, plan actions, FAQ toggles, support contact, and trial actions appear clickable.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Trust-oriented, polished, and conversion-focused.

## Ideal for

SaaS products, professional services, team tools, and subscription businesses that rely on testimonials.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce people’s identities, testimonials, organizations, logos, exact prices, proprietary text, or global shell elements.

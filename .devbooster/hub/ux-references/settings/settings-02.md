## Overview

A profile-management route for editing personal identity and public-facing profile information, with a prominent profile header and grouped form cards.

## Route layout blueprint

A wide decorative cover area and overlapping avatar/profile identity block appear first, with local actions to the right. Below are a personal-info card and a public-profile card continuing below the fold.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- profile header: cover treatment, avatar, identity summary, share, and view-profile actions.
- personal info: name fields, email, avatar upload, and save/cancel actions.
- public profile: availability toggle, username, website, description editor, and further fields.

## Components

- cover area, identity block, form card, upload dropzone, toggle, text inputs, editor, and local actions.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious at the top, then moderately dense inside bordered form cards.

## Observed states

Populated edit state with availability enabled and existing profile media.

## Observed route interactions

Share, view profile, toggle availability, edit fields, upload media, edit description, and save/cancel.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Polished, profile-oriented, and practical.

## Ideal for

Professional portfolios, freelancer profiles, and creator platforms.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the cover, logo, portrait, names, email, brand identity, or proprietary copy.

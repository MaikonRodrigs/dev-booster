- image: settings-06.png
- type: appearance settings page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

An appearance route for configuring workspace branding, theme preference, sidebar behavior, language, and banner presentation.

## Route layout blueprint

The title and internal tabs sit above horizontal preference rows: logo, brand-color choices, theme previews, sidebar toggle, language selector, and banner-style previews. Bottom actions close the form.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- branding: logo preview and replacement action.
- brand color: preset swatches and custom control.
- display preference: three visual theme cards.
- sidebar behavior: transparency toggle.
- language: dropdown selector.
- banner appearance: three preview cards.

## Components

Logo control, color selector, six preview cards, toggle, dropdown, reset link, cancel, and save changes.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and visual, mixing compact rows with larger preview groups.

## Observed states

One theme, banner style, custom color, and sidebar preference are selected.

## Observed route interactions

Logo replacement, color/theme/banner selection, toggle, language dropdown, reset, and save.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean product customization interface.

## Ideal for

SaaS appearance settings and theme customization.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce logos, exact color values, product names, or preview artwork.

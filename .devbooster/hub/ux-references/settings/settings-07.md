# Reference — Invite and manage team members

- image: settings-07.png
- type: team management page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A team-administration route for inviting collaborators with permissions and managing existing members.

## Route layout blueprint

A compact breadcrumb and heading appear first. An invite form with three email/role rows is followed by a bordered member table.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- invite members: explanatory copy, three email inputs paired with role selectors, add-another, and send-invites actions.
- team members: five-row table with selection, identity, role, delete, and edit columns.

## Components

Three invite rows, repeat action, primary invite button, checkbox table, member identities, roles, and row actions.

## Density and rhythm

Compact and operational, with clear separation between form and table.

## Observed states

Populated team state with multiple members and permission roles.

## Observed route interactions

Breadcrumb, email entry, role dropdowns, add another, send invites, selection, edit, and delete.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Administrative and collaboration-focused.

## Ideal for

Workspace administration and permission management.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell, brands, names, portraits, emails, and exact permission wording.

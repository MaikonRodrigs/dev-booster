# Reference — Paginated team directory

- image: settings-10.png
- type: team members table
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A settings tab for managing a larger team through a sortable, paginated table with status and team assignments.

## Route layout blueprint

A settings title and internal tabs precede one bordered table card. Its header contains a member count and actions, the body shows eight visible members, and pagination sits in the footer.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- table header: member heading, count badge, CSV download, and add-user action.
- member table: checkbox, name/handle, status, email, teams, and row actions.
- pagination: previous, numbered pages, and next.

## Components

Eight visible rows, active/offline badges, team tags, delete/edit icons, CSV download, add user, and pagination.

## Density and rhythm

Dense but readable, optimized for scanning many members.

## Observed states

Populated first page with mixed statuses and multiple team tags.

## Observed route interactions

Tabs, sortable headers, selection, CSV download, add user, delete/edit, and pagination.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Operational and enterprise-friendly.

## Ideal for

Large-team administration and workspace access control.

## Do not reproduce

Do not copy names, handles, emails, team names, avatars, exact counts, or product identity.

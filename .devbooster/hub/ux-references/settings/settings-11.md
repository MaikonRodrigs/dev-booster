- image: settings-11.png
- type: billing settings page

## Overview

A billing route for choosing subscription tiers and reviewing chronological invoices.

## Route layout blueprint

The title and subtitle are followed by three vertically stacked plan cards. A billing-history heading with download action precedes a wide invoice table with several paid rows.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- account plans: three plan cards with title, price, description, selection control, and one badge.
- billing history: download-all action and seven invoice rows.

## Components

Three selectable plan cards, invoice table, repeated paid badges, and download actions.

## Density and rhythm

Straightforward and moderately dense, with clear card separation and a large table below.

## Observed states

One plan selected; all visible invoices are paid.

## Observed route interactions

Plan selection, invoice selection, download-all, and per-invoice download.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clear, transactional, and administrative.

## Ideal for

SaaS subscription management and self-serve billing portals.

## Do not reproduce

Omit exact pricing, dates, invoice labels, plan copy, and product identity.

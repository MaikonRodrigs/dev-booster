- image: settings-14.png

## Overview

A payment-method settings route for editing card details, invoice email, and postal billing address.

## Route layout blueprint

The route title and billing tab are followed by a long form of horizontal rows. Card details use a small multi-field grid, followed by email, street, city, region/postcode, and country controls.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- card details: name, expiry, card number, and masked CVV.
- invoice email: email field and add-another action.
- billing address: street, city, state/province, postal code, and country.

## Components

Payment inputs, security visibility affordance, email control, address fields, split region/postcode row, and country dropdown.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and methodical, with dividers between groups.

## Observed states

Populated editing state with masked security field and selected country.

## Observed route interactions

Tabs, card inputs, CVV visibility, email add-another, address editing, and country dropdown.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Trustworthy and form-focused.

## Ideal for

Payment setup and invoicing preferences.

## Do not reproduce

Never reproduce card numbers, logos, personal data, exact addresses, emails, or product identity.

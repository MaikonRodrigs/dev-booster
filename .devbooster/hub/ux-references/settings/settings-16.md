## Overview

A billing settings route centered on multiple saved payment methods and invoice history enriched with account-admin and user participation details.

## Route layout blueprint

The title and tabs precede three stacked payment cards. Below, billing history includes download action and a wide multi-column invoice table.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- payment method: three saved cards, one selected/default, and add-new action.
- billing history: seven rows with invoice ID, administrator, users on plan, amount, date, paid status, and download.

## Components

Three payment cards, avatar groups, invoice table, default/edit controls, and download action.

## Density and rhythm

Dense but organized, with cards above an informative table.

## Observed states

One default method is selected; two alternatives are available; invoices are paid.

## Observed route interactions

Payment selection, set default, edit, add method, download-all, invoice selection, and per-row download.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Enterprise billing administration.

## Ideal for

Organization billing and multi-user subscription products.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce provider logos, card data, identities, portraits, invoice IDs, amounts, or dates.

- image: settings-19.png

## Overview

An email-preferences route for opting into product communications and choosing email behavior for comments, reminders, and other activity.

## Route layout blueprint

The title and explanation sit above four vertically stacked groups. The first uses checkboxes for subscription topics; the remaining groups use radio choices with supporting descriptions.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- product notifications: three topic checkboxes.
- comments: three radio choices.
- reminders: three radio choices.
- more activity: two radio choices.

## Components

Checkbox group, three radio groups, helper descriptions, and divider-separated preference sections.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and calm, with large gaps between groups.

## Observed states

Two topic checkboxes are enabled and each activity group has a selected option.

## Observed route interactions

Checkbox and mutually exclusive radio selection.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Readable and low-friction.

## Ideal for

Marketing preferences and email subscription screens.

## Do not reproduce

Omit brand name, proprietary copy, user identity, exact labels, and global navigation.

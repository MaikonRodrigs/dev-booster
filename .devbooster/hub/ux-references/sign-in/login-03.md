# Reference — Login form inside a bordered card

- image: login-03.png
- type: card-based sign-in page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview
A centered authentication route that places the complete credential flow inside a distinct bordered card for separation from the page background.

## Route layout blueprint
The centered identity and heading block sits above a single card. The card contains labeled email and password fields, a utility row, primary and social actions. A sign-up prompt sits outside the card beneath it.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- identity and heading: mark, title, and subtitle centered above the card
- authentication card: two labeled fields, remember-me/recovery row, primary action, and social action
- account prompt: centered sign-up link below the card

## Components
- heading block: 1 title and 1 subtitle
- card: 1 container
- fields: 2 labeled inputs
- utility row: checkbox and recovery link
- actions: 2 full-width buttons
- account prompt: 1 link

## Density and rhythm
Balanced and moderately spacious; the card creates a clear visual grouping without adding extra route sections.

## Observed states
Empty email, masked password, inactive checkbox, and no error/loading feedback.

## Observed route interactions
Field inputs, password visibility toggle, checkbox, recovery link, submit button, social sign-in button, and sign-up link.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Clean, contained, and structured.

## Ideal for
Business applications and authentication experiences that benefit from a clear form boundary.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce visible identity marks, provider branding, or proprietary text.

# Reference — Testimonial-assisted split login

- image: login-05.png
- type: split-layout sign-in page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview
A two-panel authentication route pairing a conventional login form with contextual social proof. The form remains the functional priority while the adjacent panel provides a testimonial and portrait.

## Route layout blueprint
The page is divided into two equal-width areas. The left area contains identity, a large testimonial statement, reviewer avatar and role, and a rating row. The right area contains the login heading, labeled credentials, utility row, buttons, and account prompt.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- left panel identity: small mark and label
- left panel proof: large testimonial statement, avatar, reviewer attribution, and rating
- right panel heading: welcome title and subtitle
- right panel form: email, password, utility row, primary and social actions, sign-up prompt

## Components
- testimonial panel: 1 statement, 1 avatar, 1 attribution, 1 rating row
- login panel: 2 fields, checkbox, recovery link, 2 buttons, and 1 sign-up link

## Density and rhythm
Balanced across two spacious columns; the testimonial is visually prominent but the form remains easy to scan.

## Observed states
Empty email, masked password, no validation/loading state.

## Observed route interactions
Form controls and account links appear interactive; the testimonial itself has no clearly observed controls.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Polished, editorial, and trust-oriented.

## Ideal for
Agency, consulting, creative, and high-consideration SaaS products.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce the portrait, person identity, brand, testimonial wording, or exact provider marks.

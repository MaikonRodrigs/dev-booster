# Reference — Social-proof login with image carousel

- image: login-06.png
- type: split-layout sign-in page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview
A two-column login experience that combines a focused form with a large visual testimonial panel. The secondary panel includes a portrait, quote, attribution, rating, and carousel navigation.

## Route layout blueprint
The left column presents the centered login form with mark, heading, fields, utility row, buttons, and sign-up prompt. The right column is a tall rounded visual panel: image in the upper area and a darkened information area below containing quote, attribution, rating, and previous/next controls.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- login column: identity, heading, two fields, utility row, two actions, account prompt
- testimonial column: large portrait area followed by quote, reviewer details, rating, and navigation controls

## Components
- login form: 2 fields, 1 checkbox, 1 recovery link, 2 buttons, 1 account link
- testimonial module: 1 image, 1 quote, 1 attribution block, 1 rating, 2 navigation buttons

## Density and rhythm
Spacious and immersive; the form is restrained while the testimonial panel occupies substantial visual weight.

## Observed states
Empty email and masked password; testimonial carousel appears populated.

## Observed route interactions
Form controls plus testimonial previous/next buttons appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Premium, editorial, and conversion-oriented.

## Ideal for
Consumer products and services where trust, testimonials, and visual storytelling support sign-in.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce the portrait, person identity, quote, product name, or exact rating treatment.

# Reference — Split sign-up with product preview

- image: signup-04.png
- type: split-layout sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A registration route paired with a product preview that reassures users about the destination after sign-up. The functional route is the form panel.

## Route layout blueprint

Two broad regions show a registration form on the left and a framed product-interface preview on the right. The form contains title, trial subtitle, name/email/password fields, password guidance, primary and social actions, and a login link. The preview is secondary and visually larger.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Registration panel: intro, three fields, rule, primary action, social action, and login link.
- Supporting preview: dashboard mockup with internal navigation, metric cards, chart, and table.

## Components

- Three-field registration form.
- Password guidance and visibility control.
- Primary, social, and login actions.
- Non-functional dashboard illustration.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced: the form is compact while the preview occupies a broad visual area.

## Observed states

Empty form and populated illustrative preview; no validation or loading state.

## Observed route interactions

Form controls and authentication actions appear interactive. The preview appears promotional.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Editorial split-screen onboarding with a product-led visual anchor.

## Ideal for

Analytics, collaboration, and SaaS products where showing the product supports conversion.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore product shell navigation, brand identity, dashboard data, proprietary text, and logos. Do not replicate the preview image.

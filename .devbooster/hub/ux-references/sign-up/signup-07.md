# Reference — Testimonial-led split sign-up

- image: signup-07.png
- type: split-layout sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A registration screen paired with rotating customer proof. The form is functional while the opposite panel supports trust through a testimonial carousel.

## Route layout blueprint

Two vertical regions share the page. One contains rating, large testimonial quote, author details, avatar, and carousel controls. The other contains a centered sign-up form with title, subtitle, three inputs, password guidance, primary and social actions, and a login link.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Social proof panel: rating, testimonial, author attribution, avatar, and pagination/arrows.
- Registration panel: title, subtitle, name/email/password fields, password rule, primary action, social button, and login link.

## Components

- Testimonial carousel with one visible quote, author, rating, arrows, and pagination.
- Three-field registration form with password visibility control.
- Primary submit, social sign-up, and login actions.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious, with a large proof block beside a compact form.

## Observed states

One testimonial slide is visible; fields are empty; no form errors or loading state.

## Observed route interactions

Carousel controls, form inputs, password visibility, submit, social sign-up, and login link appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Trust-oriented, editorial, and conversion-focused.

## Ideal for

B2B products, agency tools, and services where customer credibility supports registration.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore names, portraits, logos, ratings text, proprietary quotes, and shell branding; describe them only as generic testimonial content.

# Reference — Testimonial and product-preview sign-up split

- image: signup-11.png
- type: split-layout sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A registration form is paired with a large testimonial and product-preview panel. The supporting region communicates credibility and product value without adding route controls.

## Route layout blueprint

The left region contains a compact sign-up form with title, trial subtitle, three fields, password rule, primary action, social action, and login link. The right region stacks a large testimonial statement and rating above a framed dashboard preview.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Registration panel: intro, fields, password guidance, actions, and login link.
- Proof and preview panel: testimonial, attribution, rating, and product-interface illustration.

## Components

- Three-field registration form.
- Password visibility and requirement hint.
- Primary and social buttons.
- Login link.
- Testimonial block and dashboard preview.

## Density and rhythm

Moderate density: the form is concise while the supporting region is information-rich.

## Observed states

Empty form; static testimonial and populated preview; no errors or loading state.

## Observed route interactions

Form and authentication actions appear interactive; preview content appears promotional.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Product-led, trust-focused, and editorial.

## Ideal for

SaaS landing pages where proof and interface familiarity reduce signup hesitation.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore names, ratings, product data, dashboard shell, logos, and proprietary text. Do not replicate the preview or imagery.

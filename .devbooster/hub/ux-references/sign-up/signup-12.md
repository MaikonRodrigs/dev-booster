## Overview

A compact registration panel is paired with a full-height portrait visual and a translucent testimonial overlay. The form remains the route's primary task.

## Route layout blueprint

The left region holds title, trial subtitle, name/email/password fields, password rule, primary action, social action, and login link. The right region is a large portrait panel with a bottom overlay containing a quote, author information, rating, and previous/next controls.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Registration panel: compact vertical form and actions.
- Visual proof panel: portrait background with bottom testimonial card.

## Components

- Three-field registration form with password visibility.
- Password requirement hint.
- Primary and social buttons.
- Existing-user login link.
- Testimonial overlay with quote, attribution, rating, and two navigation controls.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced; the form is airy and the visual region is dominant.

## Observed states

Empty form and one testimonial slide visible; no loading or form error.

## Observed route interactions

Form controls and testimonial navigation appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Premium, editorial, and trust-oriented.

## Ideal for

Professional services, creative platforms, and visually led product onboarding.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore the portrait, names, quote, ratings, brand marks, and proprietary content. Do not replicate the image.

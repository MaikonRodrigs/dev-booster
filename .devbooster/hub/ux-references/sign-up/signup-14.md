# Reference — Sign-up with data-analytics promotional panel

- image: signup-14.png
- type: split-layout sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A registration form is paired with a high-contrast promotional panel illustrating analytics and reporting value.

## Route layout blueprint

The left side contains title, trial subtitle, name/email/password fields, password rule, primary and social actions, and login link. The right side uses a large promotional field with floating metric cards, a line chart illustration, supporting statement, and carousel arrows/dots.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Registration panel: compact form and account actions.
- Promotional analytics panel: illustrative metrics, chart, explanatory copy, and carousel controls.

## Components

- Three-field sign-up form with password toggle.
- Password requirement hint.
- Primary, social, and login actions.
- Decorative analytics cards and line chart.
- Promotional text and carousel navigation.

## Density and rhythm

Strong visual density on the promotional side; the form stays compact and scannable.

## Observed states

Empty registration form; one promotional slide visible; no errors or loading state.

## Observed route interactions

Form controls, primary/social actions, and promotional carousel arrows/dots appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Bold, product-marketing oriented, and analytical.

## Ideal for

Reporting, finance, analytics, and business intelligence products.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore product names, exact metrics, logos, proprietary copy, and specific chart artwork.

## Overview

A broad contact overview page that presents several ways to reach an organization before introducing its office locations. The primary goal is fast routing to sales, support, in-person visits, or phone contact.

## Route layout blueprint

The route begins with a left-aligned heading and subtitle, followed by four equal-width contact cards in a single row. A separate locations section follows with descriptive copy on the left, a short list of offices on the right, and a wide map panel below.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Contact introduction: eyebrow, main heading, and supporting sentence.
- Contact options: four cards for sales chat, support chat, visiting, and calling; each includes an icon, title, short description, and contact detail.
- Locations overview: two-column block with heading and description on the left and two office entries on the right.
- Map: one wide map or map placeholder below the locations information.

## Components

- Contact cards: four equal cards with consistent internal structure.
- Channel icons: one icon per card.
- Office list: two location entries with location icons, names, and addresses.
- Map panel: one large geographic visualization.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and moderately spacious. The card row is information-dense but scannable, while the locations section has more open space and a clear transition between blocks.

## Observed states

Contact details and office entries are populated. The map is visible as a large panel; no loading, error, or empty messaging is shown.

## Observed route interactions

Contact cards, email/phone details, office entries, and the map appear potentially clickable. No explicit filters or local selectors are visible.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, corporate, modular contact directory with a clear card-based information architecture.

## Ideal for

Organizations needing a single contact hub for multiple departments and office locations.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global navigation. Do not reproduce product names, proprietary text, exact addresses, logos, map styling, or brand-specific icons and wording.

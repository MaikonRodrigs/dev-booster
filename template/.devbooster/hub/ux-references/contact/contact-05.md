## Overview

A global contact route that communicates an international presence through a large world map with multiple office markers. Supporting contact channels sit below the map, followed by a locations directory section.

## Route layout blueprint

The route uses a centered introduction at the top, then a large world map visualization occupying most of the central area. Three contact channels are arranged in an even row below the map. A contrasting locations section begins afterward with a heading block and multiple office entries in columns.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Global introduction: eyebrow, centered heading, and supporting sentence.
- World map: one large dotted or stylized world map with several highlighted location markers.
- Contact channels: three evenly distributed blocks for support, sales, and phone contact.
- Locations directory: a separate section with an introductory block and a multi-column list of office locations.

## Components

- World map visualization: one large map with multiple markers distributed across regions.
- Channel blocks: three contact summaries, each with title, description, and contact detail.
- Locations directory: multiple office entries organized into columns.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and presentation-led. The map dominates the route visually, while the contact row and directory provide compact supporting information.

## Observed states

The map and contact information are populated. No loading, error, empty, or filtering state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Map markers may be interactive, and contact details or location entries may link to communication or location actions. No visible filters are present.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Editorial, global, and polished, with a large visual centerpiece and a strong transition into a structured directory.

## Ideal for

International companies, distributed teams, global agencies, and businesses showcasing worldwide offices.

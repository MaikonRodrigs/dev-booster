# Reference — Image-led inquiry form

- image: contact-06.png
- type: split contact form page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A conversion-focused contact route pairing a large architectural visual with a structured project inquiry form. The goal is to encourage visitors to start a service conversation.

## Route layout blueprint

The route is split into two equal-height columns. The left column is a full-height architectural image; the right column contains a heading, supporting line, form fields, a two-column services checklist, and a wide submit button. The form is vertically centered within the content area.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Visual panel: one large architectural image occupying the left half of the route.
- Form introduction: heading and supporting line at the top of the right panel.
- Contact fields: two name fields in one row, followed by email, phone, and message fields.
- Service selection: six checkbox options arranged in two columns.
- Form action: one wide submit button.

## Components

- Hero image: one tall architectural image; the specific image is intentionally omitted.
- Name inputs: two required side-by-side fields.
- Email input: one required full-width field.
- Phone input: one optional field with country selector.
- Message textarea: one required multiline field.
- Service checklist: six options in a two-column grid.
- Submit button: one primary local action.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and moderately dense on the form side, with the image providing visual relief. Field groups follow a predictable vertical rhythm.

## Observed states

Empty form state with placeholders and unchecked options. No validation, loading, error, or success feedback is visible.

## Observed route interactions

All fields, the phone selector, service checkboxes, and submit button appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, premium, image-led service inquiry layout with strong vertical alignment and a clear conversion path.

## Ideal for

Creative studios, branding agencies, architecture firms, and digital service providers.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global navigation and footer. Do not reproduce the architectural photograph, product name, proprietary text, logos, or exact branding.

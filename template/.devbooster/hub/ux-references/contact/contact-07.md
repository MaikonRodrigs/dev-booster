# Reference — Centered contact form with office preview

- image: contact-07.png
- type: contact form page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A centered, low-distraction inquiry page with a prominent form and a compact office-location preview beneath it. The primary goal is to make message submission the dominant route action while retaining basic location context.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered eyebrow, heading, and subtitle introduce a narrow form column. The form includes a two-field name row, stacked contact fields, a message area, consent checkbox, and submit button. A light secondary section begins below with a heading on the left and two office entries on the right.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Form introduction: centered label, heading, and supporting sentence.
- Contact form: name row, email, phone, message, privacy consent, and primary submit action.
- Office preview: secondary two-column section with descriptive heading and two location entries.

## Components

- Intro block: one centered heading group.
- Name inputs: two required fields side by side.
- Email and phone inputs: two full-width fields, with phone selector.
- Message textarea: one required multiline field.
- Consent control: one checkbox with policy acknowledgment.
- Submit button: one wide primary action.
- Office entries: two location summaries with icons, names, and addresses.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious, calm, and form-focused. The narrow form creates a clear reading path, while the lower office preview is lighter and less dominant.

## Observed states

Empty form with placeholders, unchecked consent, and populated office information. No error, loading, or success state is shown.

## Observed route interactions

Text fields, phone selector, consent checkbox, submit button, and likely office links appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, centered, approachable, and conversion-oriented with a restrained secondary information section.

## Ideal for

Simple contact pages where form completion is more important than presenting a large range of contact channels.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global navigation. Do not reproduce product identity, exact copy, office addresses, logos, proprietary wording, or brand-specific styling.

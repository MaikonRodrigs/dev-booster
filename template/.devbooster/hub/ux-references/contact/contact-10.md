# Reference — Split form with creative portrait

- image: contact-10.png
- type: image-led project inquiry page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A split-screen project inquiry route pairing a detailed service form with a large creative portrait. The primary goal is to collect project information while presenting an expressive, human-centered visual.

## Route layout blueprint

The route is divided into two tall columns. The left column contains the heading, supporting contact line, two-column name row, stacked email/phone/message fields, a two-column services checklist, and a wide submit button. The right column is a full-height portrait image aligned from the top of the route content.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Form introduction: prominent heading and supporting sentence with an inline contact channel.
- Contact fields: required first and last name in one row, followed by email, phone, and message.
- Service selection: six checkbox options in a two-column grid.
- Form action: one wide primary submit button.
- Visual panel: one large portrait showing a person engaged in creative work; the specific person and image are omitted.

## Components

- Heading group: one main heading and one supporting line.
- Name inputs: two required fields side by side.
- Email input: one required full-width field.
- Phone input: one optional field with country selector.
- Message textarea: one required multiline field.
- Service checklist: six options arranged in two columns.
- Submit button: one prominent local action.
- Portrait image: one tall editorial image.

## Density and rhythm

Moderately dense but orderly on the form side, with consistent spacing between groups. The large image creates a strong visual balance and prevents the route from feeling form-heavy.

## Observed states

Empty form with placeholders and unchecked service options. No loading, error, validation, or success state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

All form fields, phone selector, service checkboxes, inline contact link, and submit button appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Creative, contemporary, and human-centered, using an editorial image to support a professional conversion flow.

## Ideal for

Design studios, creative agencies, freelancers, and consultancies that collect structured project briefs.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global navigation. Do not reproduce the portrait, product identity, exact contact address, proprietary text, logos, or specific visual branding.

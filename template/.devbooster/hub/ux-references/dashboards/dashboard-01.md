# Reference — Content and membership dashboard

- image: dashboard-01.png
- type: membership analytics dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A populated dashboard for monitoring recurring revenue, membership growth, email engagement, and content activity.

## Route layout blueprint

A route title and time controls sit above a wide trend chart with one primary metric on the left and three supporting metrics on the right. Below are two onboarding action cards, followed by two recent-post cards and a vertical member list.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Analytics header: title, period selector, date range, and local filters.
- Performance summary: one large line chart and three metric summaries.
- Content onboarding: two horizontal action cards.
- Recent content: two image-led post cards with metadata and tags.
- Member list: one vertical list of active members.

## Components

- Line chart: one monthly trend chart.
- Metric summaries: four total metrics.
- Action cards: two creation prompts.
- Post cards: two content cards.
- Member list: approximately ten rows with avatars and status indicators.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced dashboard density with a spacious analytics header and compact lower content modules.

## Observed states

Populated analytics, populated content, and active-member states; no loading or error state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Period tabs, date range, filters, action cards, post cards, tags, and member rows appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, editorial, community-management dashboard.

## Ideal for

Membership, creator, newsletter, and content platforms.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global sidebar, account widgets, upgrade prompts, logos, names, proprietary text, avatars, photos, and exact data.

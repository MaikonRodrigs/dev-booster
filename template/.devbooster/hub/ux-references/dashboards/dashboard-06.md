# Reference — Customer portfolio dashboard

- image: dashboard-06.png
- type: customer management dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A customer-management route combining active-customer distribution, a long-term trend, and a company portfolio table.

## Route layout blueprint

A page title and local import/add actions sit above a two-part analytics row: a circular active-now visualization beside a wide customer trend chart. Filters and a table occupy the lower area.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: title with import and add-customer actions.
- Overview analytics: one radial/donut visualization and one multi-series line chart.
- Customer table: search and status/category filters above rows with selection, company details, status, descriptions, users, license progress, and actions.

## Components

- Radial chart: one multi-ring active-customer chart.
- Trend chart: one wide comparison chart.
- Filters: search plus two dropdown filters and clear-all.
- Customer table: approximately eight visible company rows.

## Density and rhythm

Dense and highly operational, with analytics compressed above a full-width table.

## Observed states

Populated customer records with several selected rows and mixed statuses; no loading or error state.

## Observed route interactions

Import, add customer, chart legend, search, dropdowns, clear-all, row selection, edit/delete, and pagination.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Enterprise customer-operations dashboard.

## Ideal for

CRM, customer success, account management, and portfolio products.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global navigation, exact company names, logos, avatars, values, proprietary text, and promotional content.

# Reference — Customer metrics and directory

- image: dashboard-07.png
- type: customer analytics dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A customer overview route showing three KPI trends and a searchable company directory.

## Route layout blueprint

The route header contains a greeting, description, import/add actions, and time/date controls. Three KPI cards sit below, followed by a compact filter bar and a full-width company table.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: title, description, local actions, date range, and filters.
- KPI row: three metric cards with miniature trend charts.
- Customer directory: searchable, filterable table with selection and pagination.

## Components

- KPI cards: three for customers, members, and active users.
- Filter controls: period, date, search, and filters.
- Table: company rows with logos, domains, license progress, statuses, user avatars, descriptions, and row actions.

## Density and rhythm

Compact and practical, with minimal spacing between metrics and the table.

## Observed states

Populated records with selected rows and mixed positive/negative trends; no loading or error state.

## Observed route interactions

Import, add, date and period controls, search, filters, selection, row actions, and pagination.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Straightforward SaaS data-management dashboard.

## Ideal for

Customer databases, account operations, and admin portals.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell and verification panel; omit identities, logos, exact values, names, copy, and company data.

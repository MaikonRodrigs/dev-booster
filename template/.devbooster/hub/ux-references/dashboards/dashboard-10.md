# Reference — Sales performance dashboard

- image: dashboard-10.png
- type: sales analytics dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A sales overview route combining headline sales KPIs, a sales trend, store traffic, and a live activity feed.

## Route layout blueprint

A title, saved-view tabs, date controls, and local actions sit above three KPI cards. Two large stacked chart sections occupy the main column, while a narrow activity list runs alongside them.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Sales header: title, view tabs, export/invite actions, date and filters.
- KPI row: three cards for revenue, orders, and average order value.
- Sales report: wide line chart with period controls.
- Store traffic: wide stacked bar chart with period controls.
- Activity feed: vertical list of recent purchase events.

## Components

- KPI cards: three.
- Line chart: one sales report.
- Stacked bar chart: one traffic report.
- Activity list: many avatar-based events.
- Local add action: one button between lower sections.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and dashboard-dense, with the activity rail providing a rapid scan of recent events.

## Observed states

Populated metrics, charts, and activity; no loading, error, or empty state.

## Observed route interactions

Saved views, date range, filters, export, invite, chart ranges, report actions, and activity links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Commercial, polished, and action-oriented analytics dashboard.

## Ideal for

Sales teams, ecommerce operations, and digital product businesses.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell, exact revenue values, names, product names, avatars, logos, and proprietary text.

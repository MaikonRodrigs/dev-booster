## Overview

A banking dashboard for comparing account balances, reviewing deposits, and managing payment cards.

## Route layout blueprint

A title, period controls, date range, and deposit/send-funds actions precede two account summary cards. A large balance-over-time chart and recent-deposits list sit side by side, followed by a cards management section.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Banking header: title, period/date controls, and local money actions.
- Account summaries: two wide account cards with circular balance indicators.
- Balance and deposits: large stacked bar chart beside a recent-deposits list.
- Cards: payment card previews with spending progress and management action.

## Components

- Account cards: two.
- Balance chart: one stacked monthly chart.
- Deposit list: multiple recent deposit rows.
- Payment cards: two visible card previews.
- Progress bars: spending progress per card.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced, with a clear four-section hierarchy and moderate information density.

## Observed states

Populated balances, deposits, and cards; no loading, error, or empty state.

## Observed route interactions

Period/date controls, deposit, send funds, chart menus, deposit list, card management, and local actions.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Structured, trustworthy, consumer banking interface.

## Ideal for

Digital banking, treasury, and account-management products.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell and upgrade widget; omit names, exact balances, card numbers, payment brands, logos, and proprietary copy.

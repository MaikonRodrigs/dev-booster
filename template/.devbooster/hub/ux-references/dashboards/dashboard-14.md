# Reference — Card management and receipts dashboard

- image: dashboard-14.png
- type: card and expense management dashboard
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A card-management route combining card overview details, spending trends, and a receipt upload/list workflow.

## Route layout blueprint

The route is split into a narrow card-summary column and a larger reporting column. The left side contains card artwork, internal tabs, balances, and a compact chart. The right side contains a spending trend chart, an upload drop zone, and a receipt table.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Card overview: card visual, internal tabs, balance/limit/budget values, and mini chart.
- Spending trend: one large line chart with period controls.
- Receipt management: upload area followed by a table of receipts with amount, category, account, and edit actions.

## Components

- Card visual: one stacked card preview.
- Internal tabs: four card-detail tabs.
- Mini chart: one balance-over-time bar chart.
- Main chart: one wide spending line chart.
- Upload area: one drag-and-drop receipt uploader.
- Receipt table: multiple file rows.

## Density and rhythm

Dense but well divided, with a narrow summary rail and a spacious reporting area.

## Observed states

Populated card and receipt data plus an empty-ready upload drop zone; no upload progress or error state.

## Observed route interactions

Internal tabs, period controls, report action, upload/drop zone, receipt rows, categories, accounts, and edit actions.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Productive, financial operations interface with clear workflow emphasis.

## Ideal for

Expense management, corporate cards, receipt tracking, and accounting tools.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore global shell, card numbers, payment logos, merchant names, exact values, proprietary text, and card artwork.

## Overview

A media-led onboarding email that introduces a product through a large video preview and a secondary watch action. It prioritizes quick orientation and human guidance.

## Route layout blueprint

A compact header places the brand on the left and login/social links on the right. Centered headline and supporting copy introduce a large video player. A prominent watch button sits below the player, followed by the legal footer.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: brand area with login and social links.
- Introduction: centered heading and two-line supporting message.
- Video module: one large video preview with play and media controls.
- CTA: one watch-video button.
- Footer: preference links, address, and social links.

## Components

- Header actions: one login link and three social icons.
- Video player: one large preview with play, timeline, duration, volume, and fullscreen controls.
- CTA: one prominent watch action.
- Footer: compliance and contact links.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and highly visual, with the video occupying most of the email body.

## Observed states

Video is paused at the beginning with visible controls; no loading or playback error is shown.

## Observed route interactions

Video playback controls, fullscreen, watch CTA, login, social links, and unsubscribe/preferences links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Editorial, welcoming, and media-forward.

## Ideal for

Product onboarding, education, feature introductions, and customer welcome campaigns.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the person, video still, brand, exact headline, product name, controls styling, or proprietary text.

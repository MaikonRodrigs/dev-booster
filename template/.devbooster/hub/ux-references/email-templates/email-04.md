# Reference — Mobile app welcome email

- image: email-04.webp
- type: mobile app promotion email
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A centered promotional email encouraging a new user to install a mobile application. It combines a product-device visual, welcome message, app-store actions, and a compliance footer.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered brand header sits above a large device mockup image. A centered welcome heading and supporting paragraph follow, then two app-store buttons. A short reassurance line and a centered footer complete the email.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: centered brand mark/name.
- Product visual: one large mobile-device composition.
- Welcome copy: heading, supporting paragraph, and follow-up reassurance.
- Download actions: two app-store badges side by side.
- Footer: divider, subscription/unsubscribe note, legal links, and address.

## Components

- Device visual: one large image of mobile screens.
- Copy block: one heading and two supporting text blocks.
- App download actions: two platform badges.
- Footer links: unsubscribe plus four legal/contact links.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious, centered, and promotional, with the device image as the dominant element.

## Observed states

Static promotional email; no loading, error, or progress state.

## Observed route interactions

App-store badges, legal links, unsubscribe, and possibly the product visual appear clickable.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Friendly, polished, product-marketing email.

## Ideal for

Mobile app launches, activation campaigns, and cross-platform onboarding.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce brand identity, device screenshots, app-store logos, exact copy, names, addresses, or proprietary product visuals.

## Overview

A richer welcome email that combines a portrait hero image, onboarding copy, three linked content recommendations, a contact note, and a primary start action.

## Route layout blueprint

The email begins with a brand header and wide portrait image. A welcome heading and paragraphs follow, then three stacked linked resources separated by rules. A closing support paragraph and get-started button lead into the compliance footer.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: brand area.
- Hero image: one wide portrait image.
- Welcome message: heading and two paragraphs.
- Resource links: three stacked content-link rows with descriptions.
- Support and action: contact paragraph, sign-off, and one get-started button.
- Footer: preference links, address, and social icons.

## Components

- Hero visual: one image.
- Content links: three linked resource blocks.
- CTA: one get-started button.
- Footer: compliance and social elements.

## Density and rhythm

Long-form but well paced, using dividers and repeated link blocks to organize content.

## Observed states

Static onboarding email; no loading, error, or progress state.

## Observed route interactions

Three resource links, inline contact links, primary CTA, unsubscribe/preferences, and social links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Editorial, content-rich, and community-oriented.

## Ideal for

Newsletter onboarding, content platforms, design products, and educational communities.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the person/image, brand, resource names, exact copy, contacts, addresses, social identities, or proprietary content.

## Overview

A centered onboarding email that uses a video preview as its hero, then offers an app download section and social links.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered brand header appears above a large video player. Welcome copy sits below the player, followed by a sign-off and a centered download-the-app section with two platform buttons. Social icons and sustainability/compliance copy form the footer.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: centered brand area.
- Video module: one large video preview with playback controls.
- Welcome copy: greeting, two short paragraphs, and sign-off.
- App download: heading, description, and two platform badges.
- Footer: social icons, sustainability/compliance note, unsubscribe, and address.

## Components

- Video player: one large media module.
- Copy block: welcome text and sign-off.
- App badges: two download actions.
- Footer: social icons and compliance links.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and centered, with a clear sequence from video to message to app promotion.

## Observed states

Video is paused at the beginning; no loading, error, or progress state is shown.

## Observed route interactions

Video controls, app-store badges, social links, unsubscribe, and possible inline contact links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Friendly, multimedia, and promotional.

## Ideal for

Onboarding journeys that combine product education with mobile-app activation.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce video stills, people, brand, app-store marks, exact copy, sustainability claims, addresses, or proprietary content.

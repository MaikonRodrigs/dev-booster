## Overview

A transactional invitation email with a single clear objective: let a recipient accept an invitation to join a team or workspace.

## Route layout blueprint

A simple centered brand header is followed by a greeting, one invitation sentence, and a prominent accept-invite button. A short sign-off and compliance footer complete the email.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: centered brand area.
- Invitation message: greeting and one concise invitation statement.
- Primary action: one accept-invite button.
- Footer: delivery preferences, unsubscribe/manage links, address, and social icons.

## Components

- Invitation copy: one greeting and one explanatory sentence.
- CTA: one prominent accept action.
- Footer: compliance links and social icons.

## Density and rhythm

Very minimal and direct, with substantial whitespace around the single action.

## Observed states

Pending invitation state; no accepted, expired, loading, or error state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Accept-invite CTA, unsubscribe, preference management, and social links appear clickable.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Transactional, calm, and conversion-focused.

## Ideal for

Workspace invitations, team collaboration, beta access, and community membership.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce brand, inviter identity, team name, exact copy, email/address details, social identities, or proprietary wording.

## Overview

A product welcome email centered on a video introduction, with both an educational watch action and a direct login action, followed by an app-download section.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered brand header sits above a greeting and introductory paragraphs. A large video player follows, then a sign-off and a two-button CTA row. A download-the-app section with two platform badges appears below, followed by social and legal footer content.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Header: centered brand area.
- Introduction: greeting and explanatory paragraphs.
- Video module: one large video player.
- CTA row: watch-intro and login actions side by side.
- App promotion: download heading, description, and two platform badges.
- Footer: unsubscribe, social links, and address.

## Components

- Video player: one large media component.
- CTA group: two buttons with different emphasis.
- App badges: two platform download actions.
- Footer: social and compliance links.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and narrative, with a clear media-first flow and a secondary activation block.

## Observed states

Video is paused at the start; no loading, error, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Video controls, watch intro, login, app-store badges, social links, and unsubscribe/preferences.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Polished, welcoming, and product-marketing oriented.

## Ideal for

Product launches, guided onboarding, feature education, and app activation.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce video/person, brand, product name, exact text, app-store marks, addresses, social identities, or proprietary claims.

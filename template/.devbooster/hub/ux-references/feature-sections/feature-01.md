## Overview

A long product-feature section with a centered introduction and three alternating feature rows.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered eyebrow, heading, and subtitle lead into three stacked rows. Each row pairs a feature text block with a large product-interface visual, alternating left and right alignment.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Intro: centered heading and supporting copy.
- Feature row one: text and checklist on the left, interface visual on the right.
- Feature row two: interface visual on the left, text and checklist on the right.
- Feature row three: text and checklist on the left, interface visual on the right.

## Components

- Feature blocks: three, each with icon, heading, description, and three checklist items.
- Product visuals: three large interface screenshots.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and editorial, with generous separation between alternating rows.

## Observed states

Static populated feature presentation.

## Observed route interactions

No explicit controls; feature visuals and copy may link to product details.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, product-led, and explanatory.

## Ideal for

SaaS product pages and long-form feature overviews.

## Do not reproduce

Omit branding, product names, screenshots, proprietary copy, and exact checklist wording.

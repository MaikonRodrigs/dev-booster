- image: footer-01.png

## Overview

A minimal light footer focused on grouped navigation links and a compact legal bar.

## Route layout blueprint

Six evenly spaced link columns occupy the upper area. A divider separates them from a lower row with brand marker on the left and copyright text on the right.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Navigation grid: six columns for product, company, resources, use cases, social, and legal.
- Bottom bar: brand area and copyright statement.

## Components

- Link groups: six.
- Legal links: one grouped column.
- Brand mark: one.
- Copyright: one.

## Density and rhythm

Compact, orderly, and highly scannable.

## Observed states

Static populated footer.

## Observed route interactions

All navigation and social/legal links appear clickable.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, corporate, and utility-focused.

## Ideal for

Marketing sites with broad information architecture.

## Do not reproduce

Omit brand, exact labels, logo, social identities, and proprietary wording.

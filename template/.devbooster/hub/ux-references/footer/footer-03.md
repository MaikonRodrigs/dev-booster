A compact footer with brand copy, a single navigation row, app download badges, and a social/legal bottom bar.

Brand and description occupy the left, a short horizontal link row runs below, and app download actions sit on the right. A divider separates copyright and social links.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Main footer: brand copy, six navigation links, and app-download block.
- Bottom bar: copyright and social icons.

## Components

- Navigation links: six.
- App badges: two.
- Social icons: multiple.

## Density and rhythm

Light and compact.

## Observed states

Static footer; no form interaction state.

## Observed route interactions

Navigation links, app badges, and social links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, product-led, and mobile-aware.

## Ideal for

Products with desktop and mobile apps.

## Overview

A full dark footer combining a newsletter strip, brand/app section, navigation columns, and social/legal controls.

## Route layout blueprint

The top band has newsletter copy on the left and an email form on the right. Below a divider are brand information, five link groups, and app badges. A final divider separates copyright and social links.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Newsletter band: heading, description, email input, and subscribe button.
- Main footer: brand block, five link groups, and app downloads.
- Bottom bar: copyright and social icons.

## Components

- Newsletter form: input and button.
- Link groups: five.
- App badges: two.
- Social group: one.

## Density and rhythm

Rich and layered with clear horizontal dividers.

## Observed states

Empty newsletter form; no submission state.

## Observed route interactions

Subscription, navigation, app badges, and social links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Dark, comprehensive, and product-led.

## Ideal for

Large SaaS and app ecosystems.

## Do not reproduce

Omit brand, app-store assets, exact text, logos, and social identities.

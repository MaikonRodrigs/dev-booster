Newsletter strip at top; brand and trust content plus five link groups in the middle; social and copyright row at bottom.

- Newsletter: title, description, email input, and subscribe button.
- Main grid: brand/trust block and five link groups.
- Bottom bar: copyright and social links.

- Newsletter form: one.
- Trust badge: one.
- Link groups: five.
- Social icons: multiple.

Dense but clearly segmented.

Empty newsletter form.

## Observed route interactions

Subscribe, navigation, and social links.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Dark, comprehensive, and promotional.

Large marketing sites and content platforms.

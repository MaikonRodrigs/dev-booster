## Overview

A landing-page header focused on communicating one primary value proposition and guiding visitors toward a local action. The composition supports marketing, product discovery, pricing, support, or resource entry.

## Route layout blueprint

The route is organized as a single vertical hero block with the heading and supporting content centered or aligned within a broad content area. Some variants continue into a large preview, chart, video, or product visual below the initial copy.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Hero introduction: optional eyebrow, prominent heading, and supporting description.
- Local controls: one or two CTA buttons, an email capture row, a search field, or a billing toggle.
- Optional lower preview: one large dashboard, chart, video, product visual, or abstract artwork when visible.

## Components

- Heading group: one optional label, one dominant heading, and one supporting paragraph.
- Action group: one or two buttons, a search input, an email field with submit action, or a two-option selector.
- Optional showcase: one large preview or media block when visible.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious, with a clear headline hierarchy and generous separation between copy, controls, and any supporting preview.

## Observed states

Static populated marketing state. Inputs and selectors, when present, appear empty or show their default option. No loading, error, or success feedback is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Local CTA buttons, email or search input, billing selector, announcement link, video play control, and any visible preview actions appear interactive. Global navigation and shell controls are ignored.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, modern, conversion-oriented landing-page header with variants ranging from editorial and image-led to analytical and product-focused.

## Ideal for

SaaS marketing sites, financial products, creative studios, support hubs, blogs, pricing pages, and product launches.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce logos, brand names, proprietary copy, photographs, people, dashboard data, device artwork, app-store badges, or exact illustrations. Treat global navigation as shell content and omit it from the route blueprint.

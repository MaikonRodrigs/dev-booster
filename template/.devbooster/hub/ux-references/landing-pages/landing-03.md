## Overview

A product-led landing page for an analytics service that combines a strong visual hero, integration coverage, performance metrics, feature proof, pricing, FAQs, and trust signals. The primary goal is to guide visitors from product understanding to plan selection.

## Route layout blueprint

The route opens with a large colored hero panel containing centered copy, local actions, and an overlapping dashboard chart illustration. It continues with an integrations grid, an outcome-metrics strip, an advanced-analytics feature section with device and desktop previews, a two-card pricing section, an FAQ accordion, a support CTA, and a final trust row.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Hero panel: prominent centered headline, supporting copy, two local actions, and a chart preview overlapping the lower edge.
- Integrations: centered heading and six integration items arranged in two rows, each with an icon, title, description, and local link.
- Outcome metrics: three large performance figures in a single horizontal band.
- Feature showcase: centered heading, a large combined mobile-and-desktop product preview, and three supporting feature items below.
- Pricing: centered heading and two side-by-side plan cards with feature lists and local signup actions.
- FAQ: vertically stacked expandable questions with a local support callout below.
- Trust row: a compact group of generic customer or partner marks.

## Components

- Hero panel: headline, description, CTA pair, and overlapping analytics visualization.
- Chart visualization: one trend chart with multiple plotted lines and a circular summary indicator.
- Integration cards: six icon-led entries with descriptions and learn-more links.
- Metrics band: three outcome metrics with labels.
- Product preview: one combined phone and desktop dashboard mockup.
- Feature items: three short explanatory blocks with icons.
- Pricing cards: two plans with headings, recurring-price labels, feature checklists, and CTA buttons.
- FAQ accordion: approximately six question rows with disclosure controls.
- Support CTA: one compact contact panel.
- Trust row: several generic organization marks.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced with clear section breaks. The hero is visually dominant, the integrations and FAQ are compact, and the pricing and product showcase sections receive substantial breathing room.

## Observed states

The route is populated and promotional. Pricing is shown in a default comparison state; no loading, error, empty, editing, upload, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Hero CTAs, integration links, pricing signup buttons, FAQ disclosure rows, and the support action appear clickable. No route-specific filters or tabs are observed.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Bold, polished, modern, and product-led, combining a high-impact hero with clear proof points and a straightforward plan-comparison section.

## Ideal for

Analytics tools, data products, reporting platforms, developer tools with usage metrics, and SaaS products that need to explain both capability and plan tiers.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the visible brand identity, product name, proprietary copy, exact prices or metrics, customer marks, testimonials, or specific dashboard/device imagery. Use generic labels, illustrative sample values, neutral marks, and newly designed interface previews.

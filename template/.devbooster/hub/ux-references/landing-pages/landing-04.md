# Reference — Finance Platform Pricing Landing Page

## Overview

A fintech landing page presenting a modern financial platform with a hero product visual, transparent pricing, feature benefits, customer proof, trial conversion, and a dashboard preview. The primary goal is to build confidence and encourage visitors to start a trial or choose a plan.

## Route layout blueprint

The route starts with a centered finance-focused hero, supporting copy, local actions, and a wide abstract payment-card visual. A trust row follows, then a three-card pricing section, a six-item benefit grid, a horizontal testimonial carousel, a centered trial CTA, and a final dashboard preview.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Hero: centered headline, supporting description, two local actions, and a wide abstract financial-card visual.
- Trust row: several generic company marks indicating adoption.
- Pricing: three side-by-side plan cards with plan names, recurring price labels, feature checklists, and signup buttons; one plan is visually highlighted.
- Benefits: centered heading and six feature items in a three-column-by-two-row grid.
- Customer proof: heading with local actions and a horizontally scrolling row of image-backed testimonial cards.
- Trial CTA: centered invitation to start a limited trial with supporting copy and two local actions.
- Product proof: wide analytics dashboard preview beneath the CTA.

## Components

- Hero copy block: headline, description, and CTA pair.
- Financial-card visual: one abstract payment-card composition used as the hero illustration.
- Trust row: several generic organization marks.
- Pricing cards: three comparison cards with features, emphasis treatment, and CTA buttons.
- Benefit grid: six icon-led explanatory items.
- Testimonial carousel: multiple customer cards with portraits, short quotes, role labels, and navigation controls.
- Trial CTA: centered conversion block with primary and secondary actions.
- Dashboard mockup: one wide product preview showing summary metrics, chart, and data details.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and commercial, with substantial whitespace around the pricing, benefits, and CTA sections. The testimonial carousel creates a more image-dense, horizontally active break in the otherwise orderly flow.

## Observed states

The route is populated and promotional. Pricing is in its default comparison state and testimonials are populated. No loading, error, empty, editing, upload, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Hero and trial actions, pricing signup buttons, customer carousel navigation, and local account-creation actions appear clickable. No filters or internal tabs are observed.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Confident, approachable, polished, and finance-oriented, using clear comparisons and human customer proof to reduce adoption friction.

## Ideal for

Fintech products, banking platforms, payment tools, expense systems, financial operations software, and subscription services with tiered plans.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the visible brand identity, product name, proprietary copy, exact pricing, payment-card details, customer names, portraits, logos, or testimonial wording. Replace them with generic finance language, abstract card artwork, neutral marks, and original placeholder testimonials.

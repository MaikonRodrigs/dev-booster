# Reference — Smart Business Finance Landing Page

## Overview

A marketing page for a business finance product centered on cards, analytics, and flexible plans. The page moves from a split hero into feature education, social proof, plan comparison, newsletter signup, and a final trial conversion.

## Route layout blueprint

The opening hero uses a two-column split: copy and local actions on the left, a large angled card visual on the right. A partner strip follows, then a feature section with a three-item list and stacked card artwork. A testimonial-style image collage leads into a four-card benefit grid, followed by a two-plan comparison, newsletter signup, and a wide trial banner.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Split hero: primary heading, short explanation, email field, and local action beside a large card composition.
- Trust strip: centered proof statement and a horizontal row of partner marks, described generically.
- Feature section: small label, main heading, explanation, and three stacked benefits beside layered card imagery.
- Social proof panel: bold statement about contract flexibility on the left and a collage of lifestyle photographs on the right.
- Benefits grid: four evenly sized feature cards, each with an icon, title, description, and local link.
- Pricing section: heading and explanation beside two adjacent plan cards with feature checklists and purchase actions.
- Newsletter panel: centered signup form inside a lightly separated container.
- Trial banner: wide emphasized conversion block with two buttons.

## Components

- Split hero content block: one heading, paragraph, email input, and primary action.
- Hero card artwork: one large decorative image group.
- Partner-logo row: one trust component, not reproduced.
- Feature list: three benefit items with icons and links.
- Image collage: one social-proof visual composition.
- Benefit cards: four cards in a single row.
- Pricing cards: two plan comparison cards with feature lists and actions.
- Newsletter form: one email field and submit action.
- Trial CTA: one full-width conversion component with paired actions.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious, with clear pauses between major sections. The middle feature and pricing areas are moderately dense, while the hero, testimonial panel, and trial banner rely on broad negative space.

## Observed states

The page is populated and promotional. No loading, error, empty, editing, upload, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

The hero email field, hero action, feature links, pricing actions, newsletter submission, and trial buttons appear interactive. No internal route tabs are visible.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, friendly, modern B2B marketing page with strong split layouts, card-focused product imagery, social proof, and repeated conversion prompts.

## Ideal for

Business banking, corporate spending tools, accounting platforms, expense products, subscription SaaS, and finance products targeting growing companies.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce visible brands, partner marks, product names, proprietary copy, payment-card details, recognizable people or photographs, dashboard labels, or specific illustrations. Replace them with generic copy and non-identifying visual placeholders.

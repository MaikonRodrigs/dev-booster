# Reference — Modular Banking Product Landing Page

- type: banking product marketing landing page

## Overview

A banking product landing page that combines a centered, high-impact hero with card imagery, feature education, a three-tier pricing comparison, integrations, customer trust, and trial signup. It is designed to communicate a broad platform offering while retaining a clear conversion path.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered hero places a short eyebrow, large heading, supporting copy, and two actions above an oversized overlapping-card visual. A three-item feature section follows with copy on the left and a product image on the right. A three-column pricing row comes next, followed by a compact benefits strip. The lower half contains another feature section with mobile product imagery, an integrations grid, a trust panel, a wide trial CTA, and a newsletter signup.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Centered hero: eyebrow, primary heading, supporting paragraph, two local actions, and large card artwork.
- Feature section: small label, heading, explanation, three stacked benefits, and a large card visual in a two-column layout.
- Pricing comparison: heading, explanation, and three adjacent plan cards with increasing capability levels and local purchase actions.
- Benefits strip: three evenly distributed benefit summaries with small icons.
- Product feature section: heading, explanation, three benefits, and overlapping mobile-device product previews.
- Integrations section: centered heading and explanation above a grid of six integration tiles, each with an icon, title, and link.
- Trust panel: centered proof statement with partner marks, described generically.
- Trial banner: wide emphasized conversion block with two actions.
- Newsletter: email signup beside supporting copy.

## Components

- Centered hero content: one eyebrow, heading, paragraph, and two buttons.
- Hero card composition: one decorative visual group.
- Feature lists: two separate three-item lists with icons, descriptions, and local links.
- Pricing cards: three plan cards with feature checklists and purchase buttons.
- Benefits strip: three compact summary items.
- Mobile product preview: one overlapping device illustration group.
- Integration tiles: six items in a multi-column grid.
- Trust indicator: one partner-logo row, not reproduced.
- Trial CTA: one wide conversion component.
- Newsletter form: one email field and submit action.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious at the hero and major feature transitions, with a denser middle section for pricing and integrations. The repeated three-item patterns create a steady, modular rhythm across the route.

## Observed states

The page is populated and promotional. Product previews show populated account data; no loading, error, empty, editing, upload, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Hero actions, feature links, pricing buttons, integration links, trial buttons, and newsletter submission appear interactive. No internal tabs are visible.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Polished, structured, and product-led, combining editorial hero composition with modular comparison cards and a capability-oriented integrations section.

## Ideal for

Digital banking, corporate card platforms, financial APIs, expense management, fintech infrastructure, and multi-feature B2B SaaS products.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce brand identity, partner or integration logos, product names, proprietary text, payment-card numbers, network symbols, device UI labels, or specific illustrations. Substitute generic labels and abstract interface imagery.

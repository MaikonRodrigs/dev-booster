## Overview

A product-led landing page for creative businesses that presents a finance dashboard as the central value proposition. The route emphasizes feature education, a free-trial CTA, frequently asked questions, app promotion, and newsletter capture.

## Route layout blueprint

The route opens with a split hero: a large text block and local contact action on the left, with overlapping desktop dashboard panels extending across the right. A feature section follows with a vertical list of three feature explanations on the left and mobile product mockups on the right. A horizontal trial banner precedes an FAQ list. A centered support panel, an app-focused CTA with mobile preview, and a newsletter signup complete the route.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- hero: large heading, supporting paragraph, local contact or link action, and a layered dashboard preview occupying the opposite side.
- feature explanation: section label and heading above three vertically stacked feature items with short descriptions and inline learn-more actions, paired with mobile previews.
- trial banner: compact horizontal conversion panel with short message and two local actions.
- FAQ: centered heading and supporting text above a single-column accordion-style list of six questions.
- support panel: wide light panel with small avatar group, help prompt, and one contact action.
- app promotion: two-column CTA with heading, supporting text, app-store-style download badges, and a tall mobile preview.
- newsletter: centered signup prompt with email input, subscribe action, and privacy note.

## Components

- hero content block: heading, paragraph, one local action, and a short supporting contact field or label.
- dashboard mockup: one layered desktop analytics preview with summary metrics, chart, and customer data; specific interface content is not reproduced.
- feature list: three stacked feature items with small icons, descriptions, and text links.
- mobile mockups: two overlapping phone previews in the feature area and one additional phone preview in the app section.
- trial banner: one horizontal CTA panel with two buttons.
- FAQ accordion: six question rows, each with an expand indicator.
- support CTA: avatar group, explanatory prompt, and one button.
- newsletter form: one email field, one subscribe button, and a privacy note.

## Density and rhythm

Spacious and highly scannable. Large sections have ample breathing room, while the FAQ becomes denser through repeated rows. The sequence moves from product proof to education, reassurance, support, and retention.

## Observed states

The page is populated in a default marketing state. The FAQ rows are collapsed, the newsletter input is empty, and no loading, error, empty, editing, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Hero and trial actions appear clickable. Feature learn-more links provide local navigation within the route. FAQ rows imply accordion expansion. The support action, app download badges, and newsletter form are interactive elements.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, structured, and product-focused, with an editorial SaaS presentation. Interface mockups carry much of the visual proof, while simple card panels and repeated accent actions organize conversion points.

## Ideal for

Business finance platforms, creator tools, workflow SaaS, subscription products, and dashboard-based services that need a long-form explanation page with FAQ and acquisition support.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the visible product name, navigation labels, dashboard data, partner or app-store marks, proprietary FAQ wording, portraits, phone screens, logos, or specific interface artwork. Replace them with original generic copy, neutral mockups, and properly licensed project assets.

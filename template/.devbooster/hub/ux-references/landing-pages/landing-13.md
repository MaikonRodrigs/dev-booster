# Reference — Analytics SaaS landing page with accent CTA bands

- image: landing-13.webp
- type: analytics SaaS landing page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A performance-tracking SaaS landing page designed to move visitors from a concise product promise to a free trial. It uses a mobile-first hero, partner proof, feature cards, pricing comparison, newsletter capture, and a high-contrast closing conversion section.

## Route layout blueprint

The route begins with a two-column hero: text and app download actions on the left, and a large mobile dashboard preview on the right. A centered trust strip follows. The next feature section uses a four-item grid beside overlapping dashboard and phone previews. A wide accent-colored trial panel combines copy, download actions, testimonial snippets, and a phone mockup. Pricing follows with a short intro beside two plan cards. A newsletter section precedes a full-width closing CTA.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- hero: announcement, large heading, supporting paragraph, and two app-download actions beside one prominent mobile product preview.
- trust strip: centered statement and one horizontal row of partner or customer marks; exact marks are omitted.
- feature grid: heading and supporting copy above four feature items in a two-by-two arrangement, paired with large desktop and mobile previews.
- trial CTA: full-width accent panel with trial message, download actions, several small social-proof snippets, and a phone preview.
- pricing: short upgrade introduction beside two vertically detailed pricing cards, with one indicated as the preferred option.
- newsletter: two-column section with email capture messaging on the left and a product preview or supporting visual on the right.
- closing conversion: full-width reassurance statement with two local actions.

## Components

- hero content block: announcement, heading, paragraph, and two download buttons.
- mobile product preview: one tall phone mockup showing an analytics interface.
- trust strip: one horizontal social-proof row.
- feature cards: four icon-led feature blocks with short descriptions.
- interface previews: one wide dashboard mockup and one overlapping phone mockup.
- trial panel: accent CTA with text, app actions, testimonial mini-cards, and one phone mockup.
- pricing cards: two plan cards with price, billing note, feature checklists, and primary actions.
- newsletter form: one email field, subscribe action, and privacy note.
- closing CTA: one message with two actions.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced with clear section breaks. Most sections are spacious and image-led, while the feature grid and pricing cards provide compact informational clusters. The accent trial panel creates a strong mid-page visual and conversion interruption.

## Observed states

The route is populated in a default marketing state. Pricing cards are available, the preferred plan is visibly emphasized, and forms are empty. No loading, error, empty, editing, or progress state is observed.

## Observed route interactions

App-download buttons, hero actions, trial actions, pricing actions, and newsletter subscription appear clickable. The preferred pricing card suggests plan selection. The closing CTA contains two local actions.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Confident, polished, and conversion-focused, combining clean editorial structure with bold accent panels and prominent product mockups. Social proof and pricing are integrated without making the page feel crowded.

## Ideal for

Analytics platforms, marketing performance tools, mobile-first SaaS products, reporting services, and subscription products that need a direct trial-oriented narrative.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce the visible product name, partner marks, dashboard values, app-store badges, testimonial identities or wording, phone screens, logos, proprietary copy, or exact illustrations from the image. Use original content and neutral or project-owned product visuals.

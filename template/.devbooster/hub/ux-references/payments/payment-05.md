# Reference — Pricing page with trial CTA and feature preview

- image: payment-05.png
- type: pricing and payment plans page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A long pricing route that moves from a pricing hero to plans, a trial banner, and the beginning of a feature showcase.

## Route layout blueprint

Hero: pricing label, heading, supporting copy, and annual-pricing toggle. The route then continues through the plan decision area and any lower FAQ, trial, benefits, comparison, or feature-preview section shown in the reference.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Hero: pricing label, heading, supporting copy, and annual-pricing toggle.
- Plan grid: three plan cards with icons, prices, descriptions, checklists, and CTAs.
- Trial CTA: wide horizontal conversion panel with supporting text and two actions.
- Feature introduction: label, heading, description, and the start of a three-column feature row.

## Components

- Plan cards and pricing summaries: repeated tier cards with generic plan names, pricing, billing context, feature lists, and local conversion actions.
- Billing control: monthly/annual selector or toggle when visible.
- Feature checklist or comparison matrix: repeated capability rows using checkmarks, values, or unavailable states.
- FAQ or support content: accordion questions, explanatory text, or support contact prompt when visible.
- Secondary conversion content: trial banner, benefits row, or product preview when visible.

## Density and rhythm

Structured and information-dense, with generous separation around the pricing decision area. Repeated plan components create a consistent comparison rhythm; lower FAQ or feature sections are more spacious.

## Observed states

Populated pricing state with a default billing option selected. One plan may be marked as recommended or selected, one FAQ answer may be expanded, and comparison rows may show available, unavailable, or text-value states. No loading, payment-processing, or checkout error state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Billing selector, plan CTAs, sales contact actions, FAQ expand/collapse controls, comparison controls, and—where visible—the user-count slider appear interactive. Global navigation and shell controls are ignored.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, conversion-oriented pricing experience with strong comparison structure, repeated cards, restrained visual hierarchy, and optional analytical or promotional sections.

## Ideal for

SaaS subscriptions, team software, developer tools, financial products, service plans, and products with tiered billing.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce logos, brand names, proprietary copy, exact prices, feature wording, product screenshots, dashboard data, icons, or specific illustrations. Ignore global navigation, announcement bars, and footer content.

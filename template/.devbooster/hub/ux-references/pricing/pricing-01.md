## Overview

A complete pricing route presenting three subscription tiers, supporting benefits, billing questions, and a conversion-focused free-trial invitation.

## Route layout blueprint

A prominent pricing introduction is followed by three equal plan cards. A three-item benefits row sits below, then a two-column FAQ area, a trial CTA with customer proof, and supporting newsletter/footer content outside the core route.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing introduction: eyebrow, title, supporting statement, and monthly/annual billing control.
- Plan cards: three horizontally aligned tiers with prices, feature lists, and primary actions; the center tier is emphasized.
- Benefits row: three evenly distributed feature/value summaries.
- FAQ section: explanatory copy on the left and an accordion list on the right.
- Trial CTA: wide conversion panel with supporting customer logos and two local actions.

## Components

- Billing toggle: one monthly/annual selector.
- Pricing cards: three cards with tier labels, price, included features, and a primary button.
- Benefits items: three icon-led summaries.
- FAQ accordion: approximately five questions, with one expanded answer.
- Trial CTA: one banner with learn-more and start-trial actions.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious, with compact plan details and generous separation between the pricing, FAQ, and conversion sections.

## Observed states

Populated pricing content; one FAQ item is expanded. No loading, error, validation, or checkout state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Billing toggle, pricing actions, FAQ expand/collapse controls, and trial CTA buttons appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean SaaS pricing presentation with a polished, conversion-oriented rhythm.

## Ideal for

Subscription software, collaboration tools, analytics products, and service platforms.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce product names, logos, exact prices, proprietary feature text, customer brands, or global shell elements.

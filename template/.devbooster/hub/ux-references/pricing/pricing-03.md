## Overview

A centered pricing route designed to explain plan differences quickly, answer billing concerns, and support conversion with a product preview.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered heading and billing toggle precede three plan cards. A six-item benefits grid follows, then a branded proof strip, an FAQ section with a side image, and a final trial CTA.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing hero: centered title, supporting statement, and annual-pricing toggle.
- Plan cards: three tiers in one row with icon headers, feature lists, and primary actions.
- Benefits grid: six feature summaries in a three-column, two-row arrangement.
- Customer proof strip: one horizontal trust panel with several abstract customer marks.
- FAQ and visual panel: four visible FAQ entries beside one lifestyle/product image.
- Trial CTA: one horizontal conversion block with two local actions.

## Components

- Billing toggle: one annual-pricing control.
- Pricing cards: three tier cards with prices, checklists, and start actions.
- Feature tiles: six icon-led benefit items.
- Trust strip: one customer-proof row.
- FAQ list: approximately four question-and-answer entries.
- Supporting image: one nonessential visual beside the FAQ.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious, moving from compact cards to airy feature and FAQ sections.

## Observed states

Populated pricing and FAQ content; no loading, error, expanded interaction, or checkout state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Billing toggle, plan buttons, FAQ rows, and trial actions appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Light, editorial SaaS marketing page with strong centered hierarchy and visual reassurance.

Modern subscription products, startups, creative software, and customer-facing platforms.

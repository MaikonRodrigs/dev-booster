# Reference — User-scaled pricing comparison matrix

- image: pricing-05.webp

## Overview

A detailed plan-comparison route allowing visitors to relate pricing to user quantity and inspect feature availability across tiers.

## Route layout blueprint

A pricing header and billing toggle sit above a horizontal user-count slider. Three plan headers align over a long comparison matrix with grouped feature rows and repeated actions. A product preview and a final trial section follow.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing controls: title, supporting text, billing toggle, and user-count slider.
- Comparison header: three plan columns with prices, descriptions, and actions; the middle column is highlighted.
- Feature matrix: grouped rows showing availability or limits for each plan.
- Product proof: two-column CTA with text on the left and dashboard/device preview on the right.
- Trial CTA: centered free-trial invitation.

## Components

- Billing toggle: one monthly/annual selector.
- User slider: one range control with visible endpoint labels.
- Plan headers: three aligned tiers with pricing and actions.
- Comparison table: multiple grouped feature rows across three plan columns.
- Product preview: one dashboard shown across desktop and mobile contexts.
- Trial CTA: one centered conversion block.

## Density and rhythm

Information-dense in the comparison matrix, balanced by open CTA and preview sections.

## Observed states

Comparison data is populated; one plan column is visually emphasized. No loading, error, or checkout state is shown.

## Observed route interactions

Billing toggle, user slider, plan buttons, and preview CTA actions appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Analytical, utility-focused SaaS pricing experience.

## Ideal for

Usage-based software, team collaboration tools, analytics platforms, and enterprise plans.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce exact prices, feature labels, dashboard data, logos, product identity, or device artwork.

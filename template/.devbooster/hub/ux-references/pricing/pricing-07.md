## Overview

A pricing route that uses a prominent product interface preview and feature storytelling to connect plan selection with the value of advanced analytics.

## Route layout blueprint

Three pricing cards and a billing toggle lead into a large dashboard preview. Supporting feature summaries and a video-like media panel follow, then a six-item FAQ, a trial banner, and supporting links.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing header and cards: title, billing toggle, and three plans with paired actions.
- Product showcase: large dashboard preview with a feature-focused heading and three benefit summaries below.
- Feature story: text-led introduction paired with two supporting benefit items.
- Media panel: one large video-like preview with a central play affordance.
- FAQ: six questions and answers in a three-column, two-row grid.
- Trial CTA: wide contrasting conversion panel.

## Components

- Pricing cards: three tiers with checklist features and primary/sales buttons.
- Dashboard preview: one large product interface image.
- Benefit summaries: approximately five icon-led feature descriptions across showcase sections.
- Media preview: one video-style panel with playback controls.
- FAQ grid: six question-and-answer items.
- Trial banner: one free-trial CTA with two actions.

## Density and rhythm

Feature-rich but balanced; the large preview creates a strong visual anchor between compact pricing and FAQ content.

## Observed states

Populated route; media is shown as a playable preview but not playing. No loading, error, or checkout state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Billing toggle, plan actions, media play controls, FAQ entries, and trial buttons appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Product-led, polished, and demonstrative SaaS marketing layout.

## Ideal for

Analytics products, admin platforms, workflow tools, and software with a substantial visual interface.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce dashboard data, video imagery, logos, product names, exact prices, proprietary feature copy, or specific illustrations.

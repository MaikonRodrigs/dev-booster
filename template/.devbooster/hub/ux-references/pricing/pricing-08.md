## Overview

A long-form pricing route that combines three plans with a benefits grid, customer proof, a visual FAQ, and multiple free-trial entry points.

## Route layout blueprint

The route begins with a centered pricing hero and annual toggle over three cards. A trial banner and six-item feature grid follow, then a trust strip, an FAQ with a supporting image, another trial CTA, and supporting footer content.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing hero: title, supporting text, annual-pricing toggle, and three plan cards.
- Trial prompt: compact horizontal free-trial CTA immediately after the cards.
- Benefits grid: six feature tiles in two rows of three.
- Trust strip: one centered customer-proof row.
- FAQ and image: four visible question entries beside one lifestyle image.
- Trial CTA: another wide conversion panel with two actions.

## Components

- Billing toggle: one annual-pricing control.
- Plan cards: three tiers with icons, feature lists, and start actions.
- Trial banners: two local free-trial CTA panels.
- Feature tiles: six icon-led summaries.
- Trust strip: one row of abstract customer marks.
- FAQ list: approximately four entries.
- Supporting image: one non-specific visual.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious, with repeated CTA pauses supporting a long scrolling route.

## Observed states

Populated pricing and FAQ content; no loading, error, checkout, or expanded FAQ state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Billing control, plan buttons, FAQ rows, and both trial banners appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

High-contrast, polished, and conversion-oriented SaaS marketing page.

## Ideal for

Subscription platforms, productivity tools, customer-service software, and modern B2B products.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce logos, brand marks, photographs, exact prices, product names, proprietary text, or global announcement/footer chrome.

## Overview

A comparison-first pricing route designed for visitors who need to adjust team size and inspect detailed differences between plans.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered title and billing toggle precede a user-count range slider. Three plan columns sit above a long feature comparison table, followed by a dashboard/device preview and centered trial CTA.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing controls: title, supporting statement, billing selector, and user slider.
- Plan comparison header: three tiers with pricing, short descriptions, and actions; the center tier is outlined as recommended.
- Comparison matrix: multiple grouped rows for overview, analytics, and access capabilities.
- Product preview: two-column text-and-interface showcase with desktop and mobile screens.
- Trial CTA: centered free-trial invitation.

## Components

- Billing selector: one monthly/annual toggle.
- User-count slider: one horizontal range control with labeled endpoints.
- Plan columns: three aligned headers with price and repeated actions.
- Feature matrix: multiple categorized rows using availability marks and plan-specific values.
- Product preview: one responsive dashboard mockup.
- Trial CTA: one centered conversion block.

## Density and rhythm

Dense and comparison-oriented in the upper half, with more breathing room in the product showcase and CTA sections.

## Observed states

Populated comparison table with a highlighted recommended plan; no loading, error, or payment confirmation state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

Billing toggle, slider, plan buttons, and preview CTA actions appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Functional, analytical, and enterprise-oriented.

## Ideal for

Team software, usage-based services, admin platforms, and products with meaningful plan capability differences.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce exact prices, feature copy, product interface data, device visuals, logos, or proprietary branding.

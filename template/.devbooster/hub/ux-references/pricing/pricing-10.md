## Overview

A minimal pricing route focused on quick plan selection, a compact FAQ, customer reviews, and a strong free-trial CTA.

## Route layout blueprint

The page centers a pricing heading, billing toggle, and three plans. A split FAQ and contact prompt follow, then a four-card review grid and a large contrasting trial banner.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Pricing hero: title, supporting copy, and monthly/annual selector.
- Plan cards: three horizontally aligned cards with prices, feature lists, and primary/secondary actions.
- FAQ section: explanatory text beside approximately five accordion questions.
- Reviews: four testimonial cards arranged in a two-column grid.
- Trial CTA: full-width contrasting free-trial panel with learn-more and start actions.

## Components

- Billing toggle: one monthly/annual control.
- Pricing cards: three tiers, with one popular-plan marker.
- FAQ accordion: approximately five entries with one answer expanded.
- Contact prompt: one local action for unanswered questions.
- Review cards: four customer quote cards with person and organization metadata.
- Trial banner: one prominent conversion CTA.

## Density and rhythm

Simple and balanced, with compact cards and generous whitespace around the FAQ, reviews, and CTA.

## Observed states

Populated pricing, FAQ, and reviews; one FAQ answer is open. No loading, error, checkout, or form state is shown.

## Observed route interactions

Billing toggle, plan actions, FAQ expand/collapse controls, contact action, and trial buttons appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, trustworthy, and conversion-focused.

## Ideal for

SaaS subscriptions, digital services, startup products, and customer-trust-led pricing pages.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce reviewer identities, testimonials, logos, exact prices, product names, proprietary text, or global shell elements.

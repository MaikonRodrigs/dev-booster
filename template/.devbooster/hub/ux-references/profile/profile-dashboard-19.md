# Reference — Profile analytics and audience dashboard

## Overview

A profile-focused analytics route for creators or professionals, designed to monitor audience activity and discover influential followers or peers. The primary goal is to review profile-view trends and scan ranked relationship lists.

## Route layout blueprint

The main route begins with a page title and two local actions. A time-range control sits above a large multi-series line chart. Beneath the chart, two equal-width ranked lists are arranged side by side, each ending with a local “view all” action. A separate conversation panel is visible alongside the route but is treated as possible global or auxiliary chrome and ignored.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Route heading and actions: a prominent profile-statistics title with two local buttons aligned to the opposite side.
- Profile views: a wide line chart occupying the main content width, with a compact segmented time-range selector and a labeled vertical scale.
- Ranked audience lists: two parallel list sections; the left ranks biggest fans and the right ranks favorite designers. Each contains seven visible person rows with avatar, name, handle, engagement count, and a small row action.
- List continuation actions: each list has a centered or trailing “view all” link beneath the visible rows.

## Components

- Page title: one route heading for profile statistics.
- Local actions: two buttons, one secondary communication-style action and one emphasized edit-style action.
- Time-range selector: one segmented control with three date-range options.
- Line chart: one large chart with three overlaid trend lines, a vertical metric label, date labels along the horizontal axis, and horizontal guide lines.
- Biggest fans list: one ranked list with seven visible user entries and per-row edit/context affordances.
- Favorite designers list: one ranked list with seven visible user entries and per-row edit/context affordances.
- View-all actions: one local continuation link per list.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and moderately dense. The chart receives generous horizontal space and breathing room, while the two lower lists use a compact repeated-row rhythm with consistent dividers and aligned metadata.

## Observed states

The analytics chart is populated with historical data, and both ranked lists are populated. No empty, loading, error, editing, upload, or progress state is visible in the route content.

## Observed route interactions

The visible local interactions include the time-range selector, the two route actions, row-level edit or context controls, and the two view-all links. The surrounding conversation controls are excluded as auxiliary or global chrome.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean, analytical, lightweight, and dashboard-oriented, with a strong emphasis on trend visualization and ranked relationship summaries.

## Ideal for

Creator profiles, professional networking products, portfolio analytics, community dashboards, audience insights, and personal-brand management tools.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce logos, brands, personal names, handles, proprietary text, profile photos, chat content, attachment previews, or specific illustrations noticed in the image. Use generic labels, abstract avatars, and representative chart data instead.

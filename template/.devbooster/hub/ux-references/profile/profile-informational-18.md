## Overview

A public profile route focused on quickly communicating a professional’s identity, background, contact links, experience, and portfolio. It is intended for visitors deciding whether to follow, contact, or inspect the person’s work.

## Route layout blueprint

A full-width cover sits above a horizontal identity row with an overlapping avatar on the left and local actions on the right. A broad “about” section follows, with social or external-link affordances aligned near the text and a horizontal contact strip beneath. Experience appears as a two-column grid of four cards, followed by a two-column project gallery.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Profile hero: wide cover, overlapping avatar, name and handle, plus an overflow action, portfolio action, and follow action.
- About: section label, multi-paragraph introduction, read-more link, and a row of external profile links.
- Contact strip: one horizontal card containing location, website, portfolio, and email fields.
- Experience: four work-history cards in a two-by-two grid, each with role, organization, dates, and a project button.
- Projects: section heading followed by a two-column gallery of project previews continuing below the visible area.

## Components

- Cover/banner: one wide decorative visual area.
- Identity row: one avatar with verification marker, display name, handle, and three local actions.
- About text: one multi-paragraph biography with a read-more link.
- External links: one compact row of several social or portfolio destinations.
- Contact strip: one horizontal information card with four labeled contact fields.
- Experience cards: four repeated cards arranged in two columns.
- Project gallery: a two-column grid of large visual previews.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and spacious. The hero is prominent, the about/contact area is easy to scan, and the experience cards create a regular two-column rhythm before the more visual project gallery.

## Observed states

The route is populated and presented as a public read-only profile. No empty, loading, error, editing, upload, or progress state is visible.

## Observed route interactions

The apparent local interactions are the overflow menu, portfolio and follow actions, read-more link, external contact links, experience project buttons, and project previews.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, professional, portfolio-led, and highly scannable, with a generous hero and clear content bands.

## Ideal for

Professional networks, talent marketplaces, creator profiles, portfolio sites, agency directories, and personal-brand pages.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce brands, personal names, handles, proprietary text, portraits, cover art, project screenshots, logos, or social-service iconography. Substitute generic profile data and abstract portfolio thumbnails.

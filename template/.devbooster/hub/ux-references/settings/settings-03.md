- image: settings-03.png
- type: profile settings form
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A focused public-profile editor for username, website, avatar, biography, job title, and alternative contact information.

## Route layout blueprint

The route title and internal tab bar sit above full-width horizontal form rows. Labels and helper text occupy the left column, while controls occupy the wider right column. Save actions sit at the bottom right.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- public identity: username and website with fixed prefixes.
- profile media: current photo with delete/update actions.
- biography: rich-text editor and character counter.
- professional details: job title and public-visibility checkbox.
- alternative contact: optional email field.

## Components

- internal tabs, prefixed inputs, avatar control, editor, checkbox, cancel, and save.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced, with large horizontal rows and ample whitespace.

## Observed states

Populated editing state with existing profile information.

## Observed route interactions

Tabs, text fields, avatar update/delete, editor formatting, checkbox, cancel, and save.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal and professional.

## Ideal for

Professional directories, portfolio products, and public profile editors.

## Do not reproduce

Omit global chrome, personal names, emails, portraits, exact copy, and proprietary URLs.

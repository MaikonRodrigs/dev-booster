# Reference — Company profile settings

- image: settings-04.png

- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

An organization-profile route for editing public company identity, tagline, logo, branding behavior, and social links.

## Route layout blueprint

A title and internal tab bar precede horizontal form sections. Labels and explanations are on the left; inputs, upload controls, checkboxes, and social fields are on the right.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- company profile: display name and public profile slug.
- tagline: multiline description with character count.
- company logo: current preview and upload dropzone.
- branding: two logo-usage checkboxes and examples link.
- social profiles: three prefixed URL fields.

## Components

Text inputs, textarea, logo uploader, checkbox group, prefixed URL controls, cancel, and save actions.

## Density and rhythm

Moderately dense and highly scannable, with divider-separated rows.

## Observed states

Populated organization edit state with existing logo and selected branding options.

## Observed route interactions

Tabs, text editing, upload, checkboxes, examples link, cancel, and save.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Corporate and configuration-oriented.

## Ideal for

Workspace branding, organization profiles, and white-label products.

## Do not reproduce

Do not copy company names, logos, brand marks, proprietary taglines, or social handles.

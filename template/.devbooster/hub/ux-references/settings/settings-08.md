- image: settings-08.png

## Overview

A team-management overview showing the teams a user belongs to and members of the currently managed team.

## Route layout blueprint

The title and subtitle are followed by a teams section with create/add actions and four team rows. A second section presents the current team in a bordered five-row member table.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- route header: title, subtitle, create-team, add-member, and overflow actions.
- teams list: four rows with icon, team identity, secondary domain, and leave action.
- current team: member table with name, email, delete, and edit columns.

## Components

Team list, member table, create team, add member, leave, delete, edit, and overflow actions.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced, with generous separation between list and table.

## Observed states

Populated state with four teams and five members.

## Observed route interactions

Create, add, leave, edit/delete member, and overflow actions.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clean administrative directory.

## Ideal for

Multi-team SaaS products and collaboration tools.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce team brands, names, domains, portraits, or global navigation.

A billing-details route combining an account-plan summary, payment-method card, and detailed invoice table.

A back link and route heading sit above two summary cards: plan usage on the left and payment method on the right. A billing section follows with download action and wide invoice table.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- summary cards: plan price, usage bar, upgrade action, masked payment method, billing email, and edit action.
- invoice history: seven rows with document indicators, dates, paid status, amount, plan, and edit.

## Components

Plan summary card, payment card, progress bar, invoice table, status badges, and download/edit actions.

## Density and rhythm

Airy summary cards followed by a denser table.

## Observed states

Usage progress is visible and invoices are paid.

## Observed route interactions

Back, upgrade plan, edit payment method, download-all, selection, and invoice editing.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Professional and summary-driven.

## Ideal for

Self-serve billing portals and subscription accounts.

## Do not reproduce

Omit pricing, payment details, brand marks, invoice identifiers, dates, and global shell.

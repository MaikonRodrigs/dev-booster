# Reference — Payment methods and billing history

- image: settings-15.png
- type: payment methods settings page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A billing settings route for choosing the invoice contact email, selecting a default payment method, and reviewing billing history.

## Route layout blueprint

The route header and tabs sit above contact-email radio choices, stacked payment-method cards, and a large invoice table.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- contact email: two radio choices with alternative email selected.
- card details: two payment cards, one default, plus add-new action.
- billing history: download action and seven rows with amount, date, paid status, and user avatars.

## Components

Radio group, selectable payment cards, default/edit controls, avatar groups, invoice table, and download action.

## Density and rhythm

Moderately dense, with compact settings above a data-heavy table.

## Observed states

Alternative email selected; one payment method is default; invoices are paid.

## Observed route interactions

Radio selection, payment selection, set default, edit, add method, download, and row selection.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Financial, structured, and operational.

## Ideal for

Subscription billing and team invoicing.

## Do not reproduce

Omit provider marks, card details, names, emails, dates, portraits, and exact invoice content.

## Overview

A notification-preferences route for choosing delivery channels for event notifications and periodic summaries.

## Route layout blueprint

The title and subtitle are followed by two groups. Each uses descriptive copy on the left and repeated event rows on the right with a three-option delivery control.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- general notifications: five event rows for common workflow events.
- summary notifications: four rows for daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly summaries.

## Components

Nine preference rows, segmented controls for none/in-app/email, and explanatory section copy.

## Density and rhythm

Compact and highly regular, with repeated rows and dividers.

## Observed states

All categories are populated with a visible selected delivery option.

## Observed route interactions

Delivery selection for each event and summary frequency.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Quiet, systematic, and utility-focused.

## Ideal for

Notification centers and workflow tools.

## Do not reproduce

Omit exact event wording, project names, account details, and product identity.

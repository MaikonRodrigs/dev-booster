- image: settings-18.png

## Overview

A notification-settings route for independently enabling push, email, and SMS delivery across activity categories.

## Route layout blueprint

A back link, title, and tabs precede four stacked categories. Each has explanatory copy on the left and three vertically arranged channel toggles on the right.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- notification intro: title and account-notification disclaimer.
- comments, tags, reminders, and more activity: each contains push, email, and SMS toggles.

## Components

Four repeated preference groups and twelve channel toggles with mixed states.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and vertically repetitive, with strong dividers.

## Observed states

Mixed enabled/disabled channel configuration.

## Observed route interactions

Back navigation, tabs, and independent push/email/SMS toggles.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Clear, calm, and preference-oriented.

## Ideal for

Communication settings and configurable workflow alerts.

## Do not reproduce

Do not reproduce names, account details, trial messaging, exact copy, or global shell.

## Overview

An integrations catalog for browsing connected apps by category and enabling or inspecting individual integrations.

## Route layout blueprint

The title and tabs lead into an integrations heading with request-integration action. A category tab row sits above a three-column grid of nine app cards arranged in three rows.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- route header: title, subtitle, request-integration, and overflow actions.
- category filters: six local categories with one active.
- integration grid: nine cards in three columns.

## Components

Category tabs; nine cards with icon, name, description, enabled/disabled toggle, and view-integration action; request button.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and card-dense, with consistent three-column alignment.

## Observed states

Nine integrations are visible with mixed toggle states; the catalog is populated.

## Observed route interactions

Category tabs, request integration, app toggles, view integration, and overflow.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Catalog-like and marketplace-oriented.

## Ideal for

Integration directories, plugin marketplaces, and extensible SaaS products.

## Do not reproduce

Omit third-party logos, names, exact descriptions, brand colors, proprietary copy, and global shell.

# Reference — Centered login form with account switcher

- image: login-01.png
- type: sign-in page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview
A focused authentication screen for returning users. The primary goal is to collect email and password while offering account creation, password recovery, and a third-party sign-in option.

## Route layout blueprint
A narrow form is centered in the page. A small product mark sits above the title, followed by a welcome subtitle, a two-option sign-up/log-in switcher, the credential fields, a utility row, primary and secondary actions, and a sign-up prompt.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- identity and heading: small mark, page title, and supporting sentence centered together
- account mode: two-segment internal switcher with log in selected
- credentials: email field followed by password field with visibility control
- utilities: remember-me checkbox on the left and password recovery link on the right
- actions: full-width primary submit button and outlined social sign-in button
- account prompt: centered invitation to create an account

## Components
- heading block: 1 title and 1 subtitle
- mode switcher: 2 options
- credential fields: 2 inputs
- utility row: 1 checkbox and 1 recovery link
- action buttons: 1 primary and 1 social button
- footer prompt: 1 sign-up link

## Density and rhythm
Spacious and compact, with a single vertical flow and clear breathing room between form groups.

## Observed states
Empty email field, masked password field, inactive checkbox, and populated login structure; no validation or loading state observed.

## Observed route interactions
Mode switcher, password visibility toggle, remember-me checkbox, password recovery link, submit button, social sign-in button, and sign-up link appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Minimal, centered, clean authentication layout.

## Ideal for
Consumer SaaS, lightweight web applications, and products needing a conventional email/password entry point.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce the visible logo, brand name, provider branding, or proprietary wording; use generic equivalents only.

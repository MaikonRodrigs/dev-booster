## Overview
A compact returning-user login flow that reduces the form to a stacked credential control and a small set of actions.

## Route layout blueprint
A small identity row appears above the heading. Below the heading and supporting sentence is a combined-looking two-row credential area, followed by a primary submit button, a social sign-in button, and two centered account links.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- identity: small mark and adjacent product label
- heading: title and supporting sentence
- credentials: email row above masked password row with visibility control
- actions: primary submit and outlined social sign-in buttons
- secondary links: sign-up prompt and password recovery link on separate centered lines

## Components
- identity row: 1 mark and 1 label
- credential group: 2 fields
- action buttons: 2
- secondary links: 2

## Density and rhythm
Very compact, with tightly grouped credentials and a short vertical footprint.

## Observed states
Empty email, masked password, and no error or loading state observed.

## Observed route interactions
Credential inputs, visibility toggle, submit button, social sign-in button, sign-up link, and recovery link.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Minimal and efficient, emphasizing fast form completion.

## Ideal for
Simple authentication routes, embedded login dialogs, and narrow responsive layouts.

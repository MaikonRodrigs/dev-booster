## Overview
A conventional login route framed by a lightweight top identity row and a top-right account-creation prompt. It supports password login, social login, and enterprise SSO.

## Route layout blueprint
A small identity label sits at the top left while a sign-up prompt sits at the top right. The main form is centered below, with title, subtitle, account mode switcher, labeled fields, utility row, primary/social buttons, and an SSO link.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- top identity and account prompt: two horizontally separated contextual elements
- heading: title and subtitle
- mode switcher: sign-up and log-in options
- credentials: labeled email and password fields
- utilities and actions: checkbox, recovery link, primary button, social button
- enterprise access: centered SSO continuation link

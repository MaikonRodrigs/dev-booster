# Reference — Email-first authentication with multiple providers

- image: login-08.png
- type: social sign-in page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview
An authentication entry screen that begins with a single email field and offers several external identity providers before the user reaches password entry.

## Route layout blueprint
Centered identity and heading appear at the top. A single email field is followed by a primary continue action, a horizontal divider, and three stacked provider buttons. A sign-up prompt closes the flow.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- identity and heading: mark, title, and subtitle
- email entry: 1 input and primary continue button
- provider alternatives: divider followed by 3 social-provider buttons
- account prompt: centered sign-up link

## Components
- heading block: 1 title and 1 subtitle
- email field: 1
- primary action: 1
- divider: 1
- provider buttons: 3
- sign-up prompt: 1

## Density and rhythm
Compact but clearly segmented, with the divider separating email and provider paths.

## Observed states
Empty email field; no password step, validation, or loading state observed.

## Observed route interactions
Email continuation, three provider buttons, and sign-up link.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Direct, modern, and choice-oriented.

## Ideal for
Products prioritizing passwordless progression or federated sign-in.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce provider logos, brand marks, product identity, or exact copy.

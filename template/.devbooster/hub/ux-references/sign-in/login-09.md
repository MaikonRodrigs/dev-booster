- image: login-09.png

## Overview
A self-contained login form presented as a raised card against an open background, intended to feel like a focused authentication surface.

## Route layout blueprint
A single centered card contains identity, welcome heading, subtitle, two stacked unlabeled-looking credential inputs, utility row, primary action, social action, and sign-up prompt.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- card identity and heading: mark, title, and subtitle
- credential stack: email and password controls with visibility toggle
- utilities: remember-me checkbox and recovery link
- actions: primary and social sign-in buttons
- account prompt: sign-up link inside the card

## Components
- card: 1
- fields: 2
- utility controls: 2
- action buttons: 2
- account prompt: 1

## Density and rhythm
Compact, contained, and evenly spaced within one card.

## Observed states
Empty email, masked password, inactive checkbox, and no error/loading state.

## Observed route interactions
Inputs, visibility toggle, checkbox, recovery, submit, social sign-in, and sign-up link.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Friendly, focused, and minimal.

## Ideal for
Standalone login pages and modal-like authentication experiences.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce the visible identity mark, brand name, provider mark, or exact text.

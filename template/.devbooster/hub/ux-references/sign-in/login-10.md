# Reference — Login beside product dashboard proof

- image: login-10.png
- type: split-layout sign-in page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview
A login form is paired with a product-preview panel showing analytical content and a testimonial, communicating product value during authentication.

## Route layout blueprint
The left side contains identity, title, subtitle, credentials, utility row, actions, account prompt, and a small footer note. The right side contains a large testimonial at the top and a dashboard preview below with navigation, summary metrics, line chart, and customer table.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- login column: identity, heading, fields, utilities, actions, account prompt
- proof column header: testimonial statement, attribution, and rating
- product preview: dashboard shell, metric cards, line chart, and tabular customer list

## Components
- login form: 2 fields, checkbox, recovery link, 2 buttons, 1 account link
- testimonial: 1 statement, attribution, and rating
- preview: sidebar-like navigation, 3 visible metric areas, 1 chart, and 1 table

## Density and rhythm
Dense on the proof side and restrained on the form side; the composition is intentionally asymmetrical.

## Observed states
Login fields empty/masked; product preview is populated with analytics and table rows.

## Observed route interactions
Login controls are interactive; preview controls are visible but may be illustrative rather than part of the sign-in route.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Product-led, analytical, and promotional.

## Ideal for
Dashboard SaaS products that want to preview value without interrupting authentication.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce logos, product names, testimonial identities/copy, customer data, or exact dashboard content.

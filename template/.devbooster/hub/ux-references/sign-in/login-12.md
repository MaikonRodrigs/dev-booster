# Reference — Image-led testimonial login

- image: login-12.png
- type: split-layout sign-in page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview
A standard login form is paired with a full-height photographic testimonial panel, combining authentication with strong visual storytelling.

## Route layout blueprint
The left column contains identity, welcome heading, labeled email/password fields, utility row, primary/social actions, and account prompt. The right column is a tall image panel with a lower overlay containing testimonial text, attribution, role, and carousel navigation.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- login column: identity, heading, credentials, utilities, actions, sign-up prompt
- visual proof column: full-height image with lower testimonial overlay and two navigation controls

## Components
- form: 2 fields, checkbox, recovery link, 2 buttons, 1 account link
- proof panel: 1 image, 1 quote, 1 attribution block, 2 navigation buttons

## Density and rhythm
Spacious and visually dominant on the proof side; the form is vertically centered and easy to scan.

## Observed states
Empty email, masked password, and populated testimonial carousel state.

## Observed route interactions
Form controls, account links, and testimonial navigation buttons appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Editorial, immersive, and trust-building.

## Ideal for
Brand-led products, agencies, and services where testimonials support conversion.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce the person, image, testimonial wording, identities, brand names, or exact provider marks.

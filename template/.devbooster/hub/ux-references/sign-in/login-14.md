# Reference — Portrait testimonial login with overlay card

- image: login-14.png
- type: split-layout sign-in page

## Overview
A conventional login form is paired with a portrait image and a translucent testimonial card, combining personal imagery with social proof.

## Route layout blueprint
The left column includes identity, welcome heading, labeled credentials, utilities, actions, account prompt, and footer note. The right column contains a full-height portrait with a lower floating overlay card containing a quote, attribution, role, rating, and navigation controls.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- login column: identity, heading, fields, utilities, buttons, account prompt
- portrait proof column: full-height image and lower overlay testimonial card
- overlay content: quote, reviewer details, rating, and previous/next controls

## Components
- form: 2 fields, checkbox, recovery link, 2 buttons, 1 account link
- testimonial card: 1 quote, 1 attribution, 1 role block, 1 rating, 2 navigation buttons

## Density and rhythm
Spacious with a strong visual anchor; the overlay card adds a compact dense region over the image.

## Observed states
Empty email, masked password, populated testimonial content.

## Observed route interactions
Form controls and testimonial navigation appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Warm, editorial, and trust-oriented.

## Ideal for
Creative agencies, service businesses, and high-touch products.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce portrait imagery, people, names, quotes, brand marks, or provider branding.

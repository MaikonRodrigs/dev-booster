# Reference — Login with dashboard carousel promotion

- image: login-15.png
- type: split-layout sign-in page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview
A standard login form is paired with a colored promotional panel showcasing a dashboard chart and a short onboarding message.

## Route layout blueprint
The left white column contains identity, title, subtitle, credentials, utility row, primary/social actions, account prompt, and footer note. The right promotional column contains a dashboard chart card, a circular metric overlay, a welcome message, and carousel navigation indicators/arrows.

## Sections (top to bottom)
- login column: identity, heading, fields, utilities, actions, account prompt
- promotion column: chart preview with metric overlay
- promotion footer: welcome message, supporting sentence, pagination dots, and previous/next arrows

## Components
- login form: 2 fields, checkbox, recovery link, 2 buttons, 1 account link
- product preview: 1 line chart card and 1 circular metric card
- carousel controls: pagination indicators and 2 arrow buttons

## Density and rhythm
Moderately spacious; the promotional side is visually dense in the upper-middle area and lighter below.

## Observed states
Login fields empty/masked; promotional preview populated; carousel appears on one selected slide.

## Observed route interactions
Form controls and promotional carousel arrows/dots appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)
Promotional, energetic, and product-led.

## Ideal for
New-product onboarding, dashboard tools, and SaaS sign-in pages emphasizing product discovery.

## Do not reproduce
Do not reproduce dashboard data, product identity, exact copy, logo, or specific visual treatment.

# Reference — Centered account creation form

- image: signup-01.png
- type: sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A focused account creation route for a free trial. The goal is to collect identity and password details, with a secondary social sign-up option.

## Route layout blueprint

A single narrow form column is centered in a largely empty viewport. The title and subtitle are followed by a sign-up/log-in switch, three stacked fields, password requirements, a primary action, a social action, and an existing-account link.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Intro: account title and trial subtitle.
- Auth mode control: two-option sign-up/log-in switch.
- Credentials: name, email, and password fields.
- Validation and actions: two password rules, primary button, social button, and login link.

## Components

- Segmented control with two auth modes.
- Name and email text inputs.
- Password input with visibility toggle.
- Two-row password requirement list.
- Primary submit button and social sign-up button.
- Existing-user login link.

## Density and rhythm

Compact and balanced, with clear separation between form groups and actions.

## Observed states

Empty form with password guidance; no error, loading, or progress state.

## Observed route interactions

The auth switch, visibility toggle, buttons, and login link appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, centered, quiet, and conversion-oriented.

## Ideal for

SaaS trials, productivity tools, developer products, and low-friction registration flows.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore product marks, provider branding, proprietary wording, and shell identity. No image is stored or replicated.

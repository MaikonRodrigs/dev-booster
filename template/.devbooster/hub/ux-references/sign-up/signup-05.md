## Overview

A registration route paired with an abstract decorative visual. The sign-up form is the primary functional area.

## Route layout blueprint

A two-region layout places a narrow registration form on the left and a full-height abstract visual panel on the right. The form includes title, trial subtitle, name/email/password inputs, password guidance, primary submit, social sign-up, and login link.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Form region: intro, three fields, password rule, primary action, social action, and login link.
- Decorative region: large abstract composition with no functional controls observed.

## Components

- Name and email inputs.
- Password input with visibility control.
- Minimum-length hint.
- Primary and social buttons.
- Existing-user login link.

## Density and rhythm

The form is compact and vertically centered; the decorative half is spacious.

## Observed states

Empty form with neutral password guidance; no error, loading, or progress state.

## Observed route interactions

Inputs, password toggle, submit, social registration, and login link appear interactive. No interaction is observed in the visual panel.

## Observed style (qualitative)

# Reference — Email-first social sign-up

- image: signup-08.png
- type: sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A low-friction registration entry point that asks only for an email before offering multiple authentication routes.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered narrow column contains an account-creation title and trial subtitle, one email field, a primary action, a divider, three stacked social-provider buttons, and an existing-account login link.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Intro: title and trial message.
- Email entry: one field and primary continuation button.
- Alternative authentication: divider followed by three provider buttons.
- Existing-user action: login link.

## Components

- Email input.
- Primary get-started button.
- Divider with alternative label.
- Three social sign-up buttons.
- Login link.

## Density and rhythm

Compact and vertically ordered, with clear separation between email and social options.

## Observed states

Empty email field; no error, loading, or progress state.

## Observed route interactions

Email field, primary action, social buttons, and login link appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, direct, and choice-oriented.

## Ideal for

Consumer apps and SaaS products prioritizing fast authentication.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore provider logos, brand marks, proprietary text, and product identity.

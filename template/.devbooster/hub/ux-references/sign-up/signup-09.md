# Reference — Card-contained registration form

- image: signup-09.png
- type: card-based sign-up page

## Overview

A conventional registration route that groups all form controls inside a raised card and leaves the login action outside it.

## Route layout blueprint

The centered route begins with a title and subtitle. Beneath them is a single rounded form card containing name, email, and password fields, a password rule, primary submit, and social sign-up button. An existing-user login link sits below the card.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Intro: account title and trial subtitle.
- Form card: three fields, password guidance, primary action, and social button.
- Existing-user action: login link below the card.

## Components

- Raised form container.
- Name and email fields.
- Password field with visibility control.
- Inline minimum-length rule.
- Primary and social buttons.
- Login link.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced and contained, with consistent internal spacing and a distinct separation around the card.

## Observed states

Empty form; no validation error, loading, or progress state.

## Observed route interactions

All fields, password toggle, buttons, and login link appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Soft, approachable, and structured.

## Ideal for

General-purpose account creation pages and trial sign-ups.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore visible product marks, provider branding, and proprietary wording.

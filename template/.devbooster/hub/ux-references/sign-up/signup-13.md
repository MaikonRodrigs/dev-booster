# Reference — Sign-up with promotional portrait panel

- image: signup-13.png
- type: split-layout sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A standard sign-up form is paired with a promotional portrait panel containing a value proposition and review summary.

## Route layout blueprint

The left region contains a compact title, name/email/password form, password requirement, primary submit, social sign-up, and login link. The right region contains a full-height portrait, large promotional statement, supporting paragraph, review avatars, rating, and a decorative directional cue.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Registration panel: intro, fields, validation hint, actions, and login link.
- Promotional panel: portrait, headline, supporting copy, review summary, and decorative cue.

## Components

- Three-field sign-up form.
- Password visibility toggle and rule.
- Primary and social registration buttons.
- Login link.
- Promotional statement, review summary, and decorative annotation.

## Density and rhythm

The form is compact and functional; the promotional side is visually dense and persuasive.

## Observed states

Empty form; static promotional content; no validation error or loading state.

## Observed route interactions

Form controls and actions appear interactive. The review and decorative elements appear non-functional.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Marketing-led, expressive, and conversion-focused.

## Ideal for

Creative, education, and service products using a strong value proposition alongside registration.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore portraits, brand names, review faces, exact claims, proprietary copy, and decorative artwork.

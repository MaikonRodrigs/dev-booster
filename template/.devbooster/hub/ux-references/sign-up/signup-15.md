# Reference — Social-first account creation

- image: signup-15.png
- type: sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A streamlined account-creation route that gives a social provider priority and keeps email registration as a secondary path.

## Route layout blueprint

A centered narrow column contains the account title, a prominent social sign-up button, a divider, an email field, a primary continuation button, and an enterprise SSO link.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Intro: account title.
- Social entry: one provider sign-up button.
- Alternative email entry: divider, email field, and primary action.
- Enterprise option: SSO link.

## Components

- Social sign-up button.
- Divider.
- Email input.
- Primary get-started button.
- SSO text link.

## Density and rhythm

Very compact and spacious around the centered control stack.

## Observed states

Empty email field; no errors, loading, or progress state.

## Observed route interactions

Social button, email field, primary action, and SSO link appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Minimal, direct, and authentication-choice focused.

## Ideal for

Products where federated identity is the preferred registration path.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore provider logos, product marks, names, proprietary copy, and shell identity.

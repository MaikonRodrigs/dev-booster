# Reference — Social-provider sign-up with portrait proof

- image: signup-17.png
- type: split-layout sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A social-first registration form is paired with a portrait-based testimonial panel. The route emphasizes both fast authentication and social proof.

## Route layout blueprint

The left region contains title, three stacked social-provider buttons, an alternative divider, email field, primary action, and login link. The right region contains a large portrait and a translucent bottom testimonial card with quote, attribution, rating, and navigation controls.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Registration panel: title, three provider actions, divider, email entry, primary action, and login link.
- Proof panel: portrait visual with testimonial overlay.

## Components

- Three social sign-up buttons.
- Alternative-method divider.
- Email input and primary continuation button.
- Login link.
- Testimonial overlay with quote, author details, rating, and arrows.

## Density and rhythm

The form is compact and highly ordered; the proof panel is large and immersive.

## Observed states

Empty email field; one testimonial visible; no errors or loading state.

## Observed route interactions

Provider buttons, email form, primary action, login link, and testimonial arrows appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Premium, trust-driven, and visually immersive.

## Ideal for

Consumer products and professional services emphasizing both social login and credibility.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore portraits, names, quotes, provider logos, ratings, product marks, and proprietary content.

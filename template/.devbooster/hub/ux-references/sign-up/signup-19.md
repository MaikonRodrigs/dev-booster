# Reference — Promotional split sign-up with review summary

- image: signup-19.png
- type: split-layout sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

A registration form is paired with a promotional statement, supporting description, user avatars, and a review rating over a tinted visual background.

## Route layout blueprint

The left region is a large promotional panel with headline, supporting copy, avatar group, and rating summary. The right region contains the sign-up title, trial subtitle, name/email/password fields, password rule, primary and social buttons, and login link.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Promotional panel: value proposition, supporting copy, reviews, and decorative background.
- Registration panel: intro, three fields, password guidance, actions, and login link.

## Components

- Promotional text block.
- Avatar group and star-rating summary.
- Three-field registration form with password toggle.
- Primary and social buttons.
- Existing-user login link.

## Density and rhythm

The promotional side is spacious and expressive; the form is compact and vertically centered.

## Observed states

Empty form and static review summary; no errors, loading, or progress.

## Observed route interactions

Form fields and authentication actions appear interactive; promotional proof appears informational.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Marketing-led, energetic, and trust-oriented.

## Ideal for

Startup products, creative platforms, and free-trial campaigns.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore background imagery, review faces, exact claims, brand marks, proprietary copy, and logos.

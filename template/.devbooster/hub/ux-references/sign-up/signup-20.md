# Reference — Image-backed account creation with testimonial card

- image: signup-20.png
- type: split-layout sign-up page
- source: vision description (image not stored)

## Overview

An account-creation route places a conventional form beside a large portrait and a bottom testimonial card.

## Route layout blueprint

The left region contains account title, trial subtitle, three stacked fields, password requirement, primary action, social button, and login link. The right region is a full-height portrait with a translucent bottom panel containing a large testimonial, attribution, rating, and navigation arrows.

## Sections (top to bottom)

- Registration panel: title, subtitle, credentials, guidance, and actions.
- Testimonial panel: portrait background and overlay card with quote, author, rating, and arrows.

## Components

- Name, email, and password fields.
- Password visibility toggle and requirement hint.
- Primary and social registration buttons.
- Login link.
- Testimonial card with rating and previous/next controls.

## Density and rhythm

Balanced: a narrow functional form contrasts with a large immersive proof panel.

## Observed states

Empty form; one testimonial slide visible; no validation or loading state.

## Observed route interactions

Form controls, auth actions, and testimonial navigation appear interactive.

## Observed style (qualitative)

Premium, human-centered, and editorial.

## Ideal for

Consumer-facing products and services that use testimonials during acquisition.

## Do not reproduce

Ignore portrait imagery, names, quote text, ratings, brand marks, and proprietary content.
